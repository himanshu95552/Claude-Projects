  1. $100

    Every $100 a typical imaging center collects goes four ways.

    Two of them run the clinic. One is the work between the referral and the payment. The last is what the center keeps.

    $31 pays for the scan and the read.

    The technologists and the radiologists. Gravity returns the report; it never reads the scan.

Questions

Questions about referral leakage

What is referral leakage?

Referral leakage is the order an imaging center never receives: the referral that goes elsewhere, the fax that never becomes an order, the referrer who quietly stops sending. Gravity closes it by giving referring offices easy ordering, live status and the report back the day it's signed.

What does referral management software do for an imaging center?

In Gravity it keeps referrers sending: Gravity Grow keeps every referrer's history and shows who has gone quiet, campaigns go to offices by fax and email, and Gravity Referral gives the office a portal to place and follow its orders and get the report back.

We already have a CRM and liaisons. Where does Gravity fit?

Gravity works alongside them. Offices order through Gravity Referral, see where every order stands, and get the report back, so the service your liaisons promise is the service the office gets.

How do referring offices send orders?

By portal or by fax. Upload the order and it's a digital order. Offices that don't use the portal keep faxing, and the order still becomes a digital order.

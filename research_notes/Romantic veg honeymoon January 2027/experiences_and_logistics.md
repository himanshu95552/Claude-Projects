Research date: 17 Nov 2026. Timetables below are mostly 2026 high-season boards (12Go listings for Oct, Nov and Dec 2026 dates plus operator pages). No operator has published a Jan 2027 timetable as such. Treat every time as "typical high season" and reconfirm about 1-2 weeks before travel. Thailand's high season (Nov-Apr) is the timetable that applies to late January. Currency: 1 THB is about 2.78-2.80 INR, mid-market, 10-14 Nov 2026 ([BookMyForex](https://www.bookmyforex.com/thai-baht/rates/dehradun)). INR figures below use ×2.8 and are estimates, not quotes. Every move is judged against the brief's rule: check out 11:00-12:00, check in 14:00.

## Thailand ferries and transfers (January high season): times, durations, prices, operators

### Takeaway
Every hop the brief asks about has at least one departure between 12:00 and 14:30 that lands by about 14:00-16:30, so the check-out-to-check-in pattern works throughout Krabi and Phuket. The most useful midday boats: Phuket Rassada 13:30 ferry to Phi Phi; Phi Phi 13:00/13:30 speedboat or 14:30 Ao Nang Princess to Ao Nang/Railay; Phi Phi 13:00 speedboat or 14:00 ferry to Lanta; Ao Nang 13:00 or Railay 13:20 speedboat to Lanta; Phuket 12:00/12:30 speedboat to Lanta; Bang Rong 13:30/14:30 speedboat to Koh Yao Noi; Koh Yao Noi 12:20 speedboat to Ao Nang.

### Inferences
- Check-out/check-in fit (all times assume high-season boards hold for late Jan):
  - Kata → Phi Phi: check out about 12:00, car to Rassada (40-45 min, about ฿800), take the 13:30 Phi Phi Cruiser or Andaman Wave Master ferry, Tonsai about 15:30. Calm, and fits. Alternative: the 11:00 Chaokoh (leave Kata about 10:00) lands at 13:00, just before check-in.
  - Phi Phi → Ao Nang: 13:00 Koh Yao Sun Smile or 13:30 Bundhaya speedboat (arrives about 14:00-14:30), or the 14:30 Ao Nang Princess with a hotel van (about 16:30, the gentlest ride). → Railay: 12:00, 12:30, 13:30 or 14:00 speedboat, 30-45 min.
  - Phi Phi → Lanta: 13:00 Bundhaya speedboat (about 13:30) or 14:00 Chaokoh ferry (about 15:00).
  - Ao Nang/Railay → Lanta: 13:00 Ao Nang or 13:20 Railay Bundhaya speedboat, arriving about 14:10-15:00. A van at about 12:00 is also possible but the car-ferry queue is unpredictable.
  - Kata → Lanta: Andaman Wave Master 09:15 with hotel pickup is too early for a relaxed check-out. Better: 12:00 Bundhaya or 12:30 Satun Pakbara from Rassada, arriving about 13:30-14:00.
  - Kata → Koh Yao Noi: car about 12:00 to Bang Rong (37 km), 13:30 or 14:30 speedboat, on the island by about 15:00.
  - Koh Yao Noi → Ao Nang/Railay: Bundhaya 12:20 → Ao Nang 13:00 / Railay 13:20. The best fit of any hop.
- A rough price guide per couple for one boat hop is ₹3,000-8,500 (฿550-1,500 per person × 2 × 2.8). Hotel-transfer add-ons cost about ฿100-150 more per person.
- For the last day, Lanta → KBV 13:20 is the riskiest finish, because of the car-ferry queue. Ao Nang (40 min) or Kata (for HKT) makes a safer final night.

## Thailand romantic, relaxed experiences: prices, best time of day, Maya Bay rules, park fees

### Takeaway
The romance comes from early-morning private boats before the day-trippers arrive and from west-facing sunsets. Phra Nang before 10:00; Maya Bay on the 07:00 boat from Phi Phi; Hong Islands or Four Islands by private longtail; John Gray's Hong by Starlight for a sunset/bioluminescence evening in Phang Nga Bay; sunset on Lanta's west coast; Promthep Cape on Phuket. Maya Bay is open in January (it closes only 1 Aug-30 Sep): wading only, no swimming, one-hour rounds. Park fees are cash, extra, and per person.

### Cited Findings
**Maya Bay rules and national park fees**
- Maya Bay closes every year from 1 Aug to 30 Sep. It reopened on 1 Oct 2026 "with a cap on visitors an hour"; you can wade at the shore but not swim. The Hat Noppharat Thara-Mu Ko Phi Phi National Park fee is ฿400 per foreign adult (฿200 per child) — [Visit Thailand Today](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/phuket-to-phi-phi)
- Maya Bay opens at 07:00 and runs one-hour rounds of up to 375 people until 18:00. The park fines swimmers ฿5,000. Day-trip speedboats from Phuket and Krabi arrive between about 09:30 and 11:00, so the first two hours are the quiet ones. The Phi Phi Leh circuit (Maya Bay, Pileh Lagoon, Viking Cave) costs ฿1,200-1,500 shared or ฿2,500-3,500 for a private longtail (up to 6 people), plus the park fee — [Visit Thailand Today, Things to do in Koh Phi Phi (updated 27 Oct 2026)](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/things-to-do-in-koh-phi-phi)
- The visitor cap is described inconsistently across lower-quality sources (375 at one time, 375 per hour, roughly 300-380). Boats anchor outside the bay and visitors walk in via a floating pier — [tisland.travel](https://www.tisland.travel/en/blog/maya-bay-otkryvaetsya-1-oktyabrya-2026) (aggregated via search; treat with caution)
- A news report gives the same ฿400 adult / ฿200 child rate for foreigners entering Phi Phi Don, Phi Phi Leh and Bamboo Island — [The Phuket News](https://www.thephuketnews.com/phi-phi-islands-under-new-special-service-entry-fees-85785.php)
- Koh Hong (Krabi) is in Than Bok Khorani National Park: ฿300 per foreign adult — [Thai Holiday Guide](https://www.thaiholidayguide.com/attraction/than-bok-khorani-national-park/). Krabi tour fees as charged: Four Islands +฿200, Hong +฿300, Phi Phi +฿400, Phang Nga Bay +฿300, "usually collected separately in cash" — [Visit Thailand Today, Things to do in Krabi](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/things-to-do-in-krabi)
- Mu Ko Lanta National Park (lighthouse) costs ฿200 per adult plus ฿20 per scooter; the Koh Rok trip adds a ฿400 park fee — [Visit Thailand Today, Things to do in Koh Lanta (updated 27 Oct 2026)](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/things-to-do-in-koh-lanta)

**Krabi: Railay, Phra Nang, private longtails, sunrise**
- Railay/Phra Nang: 15-min longtail from Ao Nang, ฿100 per person by day (about ฿150 in the evening; shared boats about 08:00-18:00, private charter after the last one). "Do Phra Nang before 10:00": day-trip boats bring crowds from mid-morning. The Phra Nang shrine cave and Railay's beaches are free — [Visit Thailand Today, Things to do in Krabi](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/things-to-do-in-krabi)
- Four Islands (Poda, Chicken, Tup with the low-tide Thale Waek sandbar walk) costs ฿750-1,200 shared plus ฿200 park fee; departures are timed to the tide. Hong Islands lagoon costs ฿1,200-1,400 plus ฿300, with "half the crowds of the Four Islands" — [Visit Thailand Today](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/things-to-do-in-krabi)
- Private charter (Krabi Sea Cruise, wooden boat Sri Su Pai 3 with toilet/shower, 6 h, hotel van from Ao Nang, Thai buffet adjustable for dietary needs, kayak, snorkel gear): the page lists Four Islands at ฿12,000 and Hong Island at ฿15,000 in one place, and ฿15,000 / ฿20,000 (up to 8 people) in another, so the page is internally inconsistent. A sunset option returns after dark, with a chance of bioluminescent plankton — [Krabi Sea Cruise](https://krabiseacruise.com/private-boat-rental/)
- Marketplace private longtail trips from Ao Nang (Hong or 4 Islands, half/full day, including a 5-h sunrise 4-Islands option) run about US$231-274 — [Expedia listing](https://www.expedia.com/things-to-do/from-ao-nang-private-luxury-longtail-boat.a47989678.activity-details) (search-snippet figures; per-boat versus per-person not confirmed)
- Sunrise at Din Daeng Doi viewpoint (20-30 min from Ao Nang): gates open 05:30, sunrise about 06:00-06:30, ฿50-100. Pai Plong cove, reached by the Monkey Trail from Ao Nang (10-15 min), is "the most private sunset within reach of Ao Nang's restaurants". Ao Thalane mangrove kayaking (tandem kayaks, unhurried) costs ฿900-1,200 per half day. Seven Islands sunset and plankton tours leave in the afternoon from Ao Nang — [Visit Thailand Today](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/things-to-do-in-krabi)

**Phang Nga Bay at sunset (John Gray's Sea Canoe)**
- The Hong by Starlight evening excursion includes sea caves and hongs, bioluminescent water, a Loi Krathong-style ceremony and James Bond Island, "from ฿3,950" per person, daily departure. The private charter version costs "from ฿62,750". Hotel round trip, national park fees, light lunch and dinner are included. Dietary requirements are taken in advance (relevant for vegetarians) — [John Gray's Sea Canoe](https://johngray-seacanoe.com/)
- Generic Phang Nga Bay sea-canoe days cost ฿1,400-2,800 plus a ฿300 park fee; the bay is sheltered and runs "in almost any weather" — [Visit Thailand Today, Things to do in Phuket](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/things-to-do-in-phuket)

**Phuket sunsets and Koh Yao Noi**
- Promthep Cape sunset is free; it falls between 18:05 and 18:50 through the year (18:15 in December) and the car park "fills by 17:45" in high season. A 10-min walk down the headland path avoids the coach crowd. The Kata/Karon viewpoint is a golden-hour stop at about 17:00 (the sun sets behind the headland there). Kata Noi and Nai Harn are the calmest bays. A slow day on Koh Yao Noi is suggested as a quiet out-of-Phuket day — [Visit Thailand Today, Things to do in Phuket](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/things-to-do-in-phuket)

**Koh Lanta**
- Lanta Old Town (east coast) is free: one street of wooden shophouses on stilts, 2-3 h. The weekend street market runs Sat-Sun 11:00-22:00, and the local Sunday morning market about 08:00-11:00. A late lunch over the water is the suggestion — [Visit Thailand Today, Things to do in Koh Lanta](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/things-to-do-in-koh-lanta)
- Every main Lanta beach faces west for sunset; arrive "about an hour before sunset". Khlong Khong has beach bars; Long Beach stays swimmable at low tide. The 4 Islands/Emerald Cave longtail costs about ฿1,000 + ฿200 park fee (7-8 h). Koh Rok speedboat costs ฿1,600-2,200 + ฿400. Lanta Thai Cookery School offers vegetarian/vegan versions. Faikham Lanta traditional Thai massage in Sala Dan is open 10:00-21:30 — [Visit Thailand Today](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/things-to-do-in-koh-lanta)

**Beach dinners**
- Rosewood Phuket: candlelit beach table with a personal chef, ฿25,000 per couple (≈₹70,000), from 16:30, selected months — [Rosewood Phuket](https://www.rosewoodhotels.com/en/phuket/dining/private-dining) (via search snippet)
- A private Panwa Cape beach dinner is listed at about US$251 for two — [The Abroad Guide](https://theabroadguide.com/?p=3176092) (via search snippet)

### Inferences
- The best romantic structure is one morning boat (07:00-08:00) and one sunset per stop, with afternoons left free.
- Sleeping on Phi Phi for 1-2 nights enables the 07:00 Maya Bay round. Day trips from Phuket or Krabi arrive at peak crowd (09:30-11:00).
- A private Hong Islands or Four Islands charter from Ao Nang (about ฿12,000-15,000 per boat ≈ ₹34,000-42,000, or US$230-275 on marketplaces) is the standout "private longtail at sunrise" option. Add ฿200-300 per person in park fees in cash unless the operator says they are included.
- For vegetarians, John Gray's and Krabi Sea Cruise both say they will adjust menus; request this at booking.

## Sri Lanka: Kandy-Ella train status and booking, drivers, drive times, romantic experiences

### Takeaway
Major flag: the classic Kandy → Ella train is not running end to end. Cyclone Ditwah (Nov 2025) cut the up-country line. As of the latest reports, the Nanu Oya/Ambewela → Ella → Badulla section runs (since 20 Jun 2026) and Colombo → Peradeniya reopened on 2 Oct 2026. Rambukkana/Peradeniya → Nanu Oya, including Kandy itself, had no confirmed reopening date. Officials have promised "within this year" and "early 2027". A Jan 2027 honeymoon should plan a private car to Nanu Oya or Ambewela and the scenic train from there to Ella, or drop the train. Reserved seats open 30 days ahead online, and every passenger's passport number is needed.

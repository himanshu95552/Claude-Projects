# Romance and relaxation fit, 23-31 January 2027: Thailand Andaman coast, Sri Lanka, Maldives local islands, Andaman Islands (India), Vietnam

Scope: an Indian couple flying from Delhi, wanting a romantic, relaxed trip with no nightlife and no packed adventure. Current as of 17 Nov 2026.

Method notes for the report writer:
- weatherspark.com and maldives.com could not be fetched (the session's network blocked them), so their figures appear only as search-result summaries.
- No Thai Meteorological Department station table for Phuket was retrieved. The climate figures below come from climate aggregators and travel-climate sites, and they disagree. Ranges are given wherever they conflict.
- Calendar facts were checked by computation: 23 Jan 2027 is a Saturday, 26 Jan 2027 (Republic Day) is a Tuesday, 31 Jan 2027 is a Sunday and 6 Feb 2027 is a Saturday.

## 1. Late-January climate and sea conditions per destination

### Takeaway
The Maldives, Havelock and the Thai Andaman coast (Phuket, Krabi, Lanta) are all in their dry season in late January, with a sea around 27-28°C. The Maldives and Havelock add the year's best underwater visibility. Sri Lanka's south and west coasts are dry, but the hill country can bring mist and drizzle. Vietnam is the weak fit: the north (Hanoi, Ha Long) is cool and foggy, and Hoi An's sea is usually rough and cloudy after the rains.

### Cited Findings
**Thailand, Andaman coast (Phuket, Krabi, Koh Lanta)**
- Sea temperature is about 28°C in January in both Phuket and Krabi, and sources agree on it. — [Weather2Travel Phuket Jan](https://weather2travel.com/thailand/phuket/january/); [Travelscoop Krabi](https://travelscoop.co.uk/weather-january/thailand/krabi)
- Sunshine estimates run from about 8 h/day (a Krabi figure) and 9 h/day (Phuket) up to 11-12 h/day in another Phuket source. — [Travelscoop Krabi](https://travelscoop.co.uk/weather-january/thailand/krabi); [Phuket101](https://phuket101.net/phuket-weather-in-january); [Vialala](https://www.vialala.com/en/blog/phuket-en-janvier)
- Highs are about 31-33°C and lows about 23-25°C; one Phuket source gives a 32.7°C day average and a 24.5°C night average. — [Thailand Magazine](https://thailandmagazine.com/weather-and-climate-thailand/weather-in-january/); [Climate-Data.org Thailand Jan](https://en.climate-data.org/asia/thailand-202/c/january-1/)
- Phuket January rainfall: most guides give about 35-40 mm on about 4-7 days. A climate-zone table citing 1991-2020 normals gives 6 days of measurable rain. Some aggregator figures go as high as ~176-195 mm, and these look like outliers or a different station or method. — [Climate-Zone Phuket](https://www.climate-zone.com/climate/th/phuket); [Weather2Travel Phuket climate](https://www.weather2travel.com/climate-guides/thailand/phuket.php); [Honeymoon Guide Phuket Jan](https://mail.honeymoonguide.com.au/asia/phuket-weather-january.html)
- The Thai Meteorological Department's 1991-2020 normals define a rainy day as ≥0.1 mm. The Phuket page of the TMD document was not retrieved. — [TMD climate normal PDF](https://ubonmet.tmd.go.th/files/MetInfo/climate_normal.pdf)
- Phi Phi in January is "peak dry season" with turquoise water. — [WhenVerdict Phi Phi Jan](https://whenverdict.com/visit/koh-phi-phi/in/january/)

**Sri Lanka: south and west coast vs hill country**
- January is one of the driest months for most of the island, and the south and west coasts get very little rain. — [Suite Lanka month-by-month](https://www.suitelanka.com/weather-in-sri-lanka-month-by-month); [Rickshaw Travel Jan](https://www.rickshawtravel.co.uk/sri-lanka/information/when-to-go/january/)
- The Maha (northeast) monsoon affects the northeast from October to January. The east coast is still in transition, with occasional showers early in the month. — [Kimkim Sri Lanka Jan](https://kimkim.com/c/sri-lanka-in-january-travel-tips-weather-and-more); [On The Go Tours](https://www.onthegotours.com/Sri-Lanka/Guides/Best-Time-to-Visit)
- Tangalle and the far south-east have some of the lowest annual rainfall in Sri Lanka. — [i-escape Tangalle guide](https://tempwww.i-escape.com/tangalle-south-coast/guide)
- Hill country (Kandy, Ella) in January has mild days and cool nights, with misty mornings and occasional drizzle, and daytime highs of about 18-25°C. One traveller reported landslides onto the road around Ella. One guide says the hill country gets its heaviest rain in this period, so the sources conflict. — [Holidify Sri Lanka Jan](https://www.holidify.com/pages/sri-lanka-in-january-2624.html); [Fodor's forum](https://www.fodors.com/community/asia/a-question-concerning-sri-lankas-rainfall-in-jan-1025832-printthread/); [Follow Alice](https://followalice.com/knowledge/sri-lanka-weather)

**Maldives (North and South Malé atolls)**
- January is the core of the dry northeast monsoon (Iruvai), with roughly 50-75 mm of rain on 6-7 days and short, light showers. — [Holiday-Weather Maldives Jan](https://www.holiday-weather.com/maldives/averages/january/); [Weather2Travel Maldives climate](https://www.weather2travel.com/maldives/climate)
- Sunshine is about 9-10 h/day. maldives.com attributes this to the Maldives Meteorological Service 1991-2020 baseline; the page was seen only via a search summary. — [maldives.com weather](https://www.maldives.com/weather)
- Air is about 26-30°C and the sea about 28°C. — [Resort Life Maldives Jan](https://resortlife.travel/maldives-in/january)
- NE winds are light to moderate, seas are generally calm, and underwater visibility is often the year's best (about 25-30 m+) because plankton is low. These visibility figures come from the travel industry, not from measurements. — [Maldives Tour Guide Jan](https://maldivestour.guide/articles/maldives-in-january.html)
- Year-to-year variability is real: Malé had record rainfall on 3 Jan 2025, and the monsoon onset that year was 2 Jan. — [Maldives Independent](https://maldivesindependent.com/environment/maldives-navigates-shifting-monsoons-and-weather-extremes-c92e)
- The official source is the Maldives Meteorological Service (outlook downloads exist), but no January table was retrieved. — [meteorology.gov.mv](https://www.meteorology.gov.mv/single-news/30)

**Andaman Islands (Havelock / Swaraj Dweep)**
- Mid-January to mid-May is called the best weather, often with the best diving conditions. The monsoon arrives around late May. — [Sygic Havelock guide](https://guides.travel.sygic.com/production/en/Havelock_Island)
- In January Radhanagar Beach has a sea temperature of about 27°C and very little rain (about 0.2 cm on about 4 rainy days). — [Beach-Weather Radhanagar Jan](https://beach-weather.com/Southern-Asia/India/Andaman-Islands/Havelock-Island/averages/Radhanagar-Beach/January/)
- January underwater visibility is estimated at 15-30 m, or 20-25 m as "best of the year" in winter, by the sources listed here. — [TravelTriangle Andaman Jan](https://traveltriangle.com/blog/things-to-do-in-andaman-in-january/); [TravelTriangle winter in Andaman](https://traveltriangle.com/blog/winter-in-andaman/)

**Vietnam: Hanoi, Ha Long Bay, Hoi An**
- Ha Long in January: averages of about 15-19°C, lows of about 13-14°C, and possibly as low as 10°C. Fog is the most consistent complaint, cutting visibility to a few hundred metres at times, though it often clears by midday. Persistent drizzle can last hours. — [Cruise in Halong Bay Jan](https://cruiseinhalongbay.com/before-you-go/halong-bay-in-january); [LocalVietnam Halong Jan](https://localvietnam.com/what-to-expect/halong-bay-in-january); [VietnamTourism.com Jan review](https://www.vietnamtourism.com/en/january-in-halong-bay-weather-honest-review-and-practical-tips)
- Ha Long sea and cruises: fog and cold limit swimming and kayaking, and travellers are advised to confirm the cabin has heating. Sources conflict on sea state: one calls it calm, another says NE-monsoon seas can suspend cruises for days and rates January a month to avoid. — [Bhaya Cruises Jan](https://bhayacruises.com/blog/halong-bay-in-january/); [Vinpearl Ha Long Jan](https://vinpearl.com/en/ha-long-bay-weather-in-january)
- Hoi An in January: rain is lighter than in Nov-Dec but still comes as showers. Totals vary by source: ~92 mm/10 days, 112 mm/12 days, or ~286 mm. The sea is described as rough and the weather as often cloudy, so January suits the old town more than the beach. One page calls it "peak season… perfect for swimming", which conflicts with the rest. — [LocalVietnam Hoi An Jan](https://localvietnam.com/quang-nam/hoi-an/hoi-an-january-weather/); [AgateTravel Hoi An Jan](https://www.agatetravel.com/vietnam/hoi-an/weather-in-january.html); [Jungle Boss Hoi An weather](https://junglebosstours.com/explorer/tourism-blog/hoi-an-weather); contradicted by [Gotripzi](https://www.gotripzi.com/destinations/hoi-an-vn/weather/january)

### Inferences
- **Sea-and-sun reliability for 23-31 Jan, best to worst:** Maldives ≈ Havelock ≈ Thai Andaman coast > Sri Lanka south/west coast > Vietnam.
  - The first three are firmly dry, with calm, warm water.
  - Sri Lanka's south coast is dry, but some beaches there have strong surf (see section 3).
  - Vietnam has no warm, calm beach on this route in January.
- **Sri Lanka:** for a relaxed couple, the beach is the reliable part. Adding Kandy or Ella brings cooler, possibly misty and drizzly days and long drives (see section 4).
- **Vietnam:** Hanoi plus a Ha Long cruise plus Hoi An gives a cool, grey north and a cloudy, rough-sea centre. Sunset decks and swimming, the romantic set-pieces, are least reliable here.

### Gaps
- No official TMD, Sri Lanka Met Dept, MMS or IMD (Port Blair) January station tables were retrieved. Weather Spark and maldives.com were blocked by the session network, so they need to be allowed in the session's network settings before they can be fetched.
- No measured wave or sea-state data was found for Koh Lanta, Kata Noi, Radhanagar or Tangalle in January.
- No sourced sunshine-hour figures were found for Sri Lanka, Havelock or Hanoi.

## 2. Crowds and events during 23-31 January 2027

### Takeaway
The whole trip overlaps the Republic Day long weekend (Sat 23 to Tue 26 Jan 2027). In 2026 Thailand was the top international pick for Indians over that weekend, and Andaman was a top domestic one. Expect the most Indian tourists at Phuket, Krabi and Phi Phi hubs and at Havelock in the first half of the trip. Chinese New Year 2027 is Sat 6 Feb, after the trip. January is high season everywhere except Vietnam's north.

### Cited Findings

New Year's Eve is 5 Feb, and the Lantern Festival ends the season on 20 Feb. — [ChinaHighlights](https://chinahighlights.com/travelguide/festivals/when-chinese-new-year.htm); [Farmers' Almanac](https://www.farmersalmanac.com/when-chinese-new-year); [ExamineChina](https://www.examinechina.com/chinese-new-year-2027/)

International air bookings rose 73%, and Agoda reported outbound travel up 63% for its 23-26 Jan window. — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/republic-day-long-weekend-triggers-robust-travel-activity-in-india-abroad-126012201176_1.html); [AAP / Agoda release](https://www.aap.com.au/aapreleases/cision20260127ae71748); [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/news/business/r-day-weekend-goa-thailand-vietnam-break-hotspots)

### Inferences
- **Republic Day 2027 calendar:** it falls on a Tuesday, so many Indian travellers will likely take Monday 25 Jan off for a Sat-Tue break. Expect the Indian-tourist peak on 23-26 Jan, easing from 27 Jan, at Thai hubs (Phuket, Krabi, Phi Phi day trips), Havelock, Port Blair ferries, and to a lesser degree Sri Lanka and the Maldives.
  - This suggests putting the busiest or most transit-heavy part first, or going straight to a quiet island.
  - It also argues for booking ferries and flights early. This is an inference from the 2026 pattern; no 2027 data exists yet.
- **Chinese New Year:** 23-31 Jan sits about 1-2 weeks before CNY, so the main Chinese holiday surge (Thailand, Vietnam, Maldives) will probably fall just after the trip. The size of any pre-holiday ramp-up was not sourced.
- **Crowds in the far-flung quiet spots:** in a peak-season week, distance is what keeps a place quiet. That points to Koh Yao Noi, Lanta's southern beaches, Dhigurah, Fulhadhoo, Ukulhas and Tangalle, rather than to more famous beaches.

## 3. The quieter, romantic areas within each destination

### Takeaway
- **Thailand:** Koh Yao Noi (quietest) and Koh Lanta (quiet, with more services) are the strongest picks; Kata and Karon are a middle option and Patong is a mismatch.
- **Sri Lanka:** Tangalle and Hiriketiya are the quiet end of the south coast; Unawatuna has calm swimming but a busy strip, and Weligama and Ahangama are surf hubs.
- **Maldives:** Dhigurah, Ukulhas and Fulhadhoo beat Maafushi and Thoddoo for couples.
- **Havelock:** Radhanagar (at sunset) and Kalapathar.

### Cited Findings
**Thailand**
- Koh Yao Noi is rated best for couples and slow travellers; nightlife is a few beach bars with acoustic music or "just the sound of the waves". It still gets more visitors than Koh Yao Yai, which is extremely quiet. — [Visit Thailand Today, Koh Yao Noi](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/koh-yao-noi-guide); [Explorer Tom, Yao Yai vs Yao Noi](https://explorertom.com/en/koh-yao-noi-or-koh-yao-yai/)
- Koh Lanta is the easiest quiet option, with more infrastructure, long uncrowded beaches and an "escape-island feel". — [Thai Holiday Guide, quiet islands](https://www.thaiholidayguide.com/quiet-thai-islands/); [Vietlong Travel, romantic Thai islands](https://vietlongtravel.com/news/thailands-travel-guide/romantic-islands-in-thailand-for-honeymoon-where-to-go-why)
- Patong is crowded, with nightlife and water sports. One guide calls a honeymoon couple in "Patong Beach's neon chaos" a mismatch. — [TravelTourister Thai islands 2026](https://www.traveltourister.com/?p=8153); [The Restless Beans, best Thai island](https://therestlessbeans.com/best-thai-island-to-visit/)
- Kata and Karon are calmer than Patong, family-friendly and better for relaxing. — [TravelTourister](https://www.traveltourister.com/?p=8153)

**Sri Lanka**
- Tangalle is quieter and away from the crowds, and it is not a surf beach, so it is rarely crowded. Its open beaches can have strong surf and currents. — [Beyond Wild Places, Tangalle](https://beyondwildplaces.com/best-things-to-do-in-tangalle/); [Rickshaw Travel beaches](https://rickshawtravel.co.uk/sri-lanka/blog/sun-surf-sea-turtles-finding-your-perfect-sri-lanka-beach)
- Hiriketiya is called the "prettiest small bay". — [Ministry of Villas beach guide](https://www.ministryofvillas.com/a-beach-by-beach-guide-to-sri-lanka/)
- Ahangama (Kabalana) is peaceful early in the morning and livelier from mid-morning, when surf camps arrive. Secret Beach is quiet but tight at some tides. — [Salt in our Hair, Sri Lanka beaches](https://www.saltinourhair.com/sri-lanka/sri-lanka-beaches/?cpage=2)
- Weligama is one of the most crowded spots for surf lessons; its southern end toward Mirissa is quieter. — [The Surf Atlas, Weligama](https://thesurfatlas.com/?p=20310)
- Unawatuna has calmer, reef-protected water, but its café strip is busy and loud at sunset. — [Salt in our Hair](https://www.saltinourhair.com/sri-lanka/sri-lanka-beaches/?cpage=2)

**Maldives**
- Dhigurah has a quiet bikini beach away from the village and a long sandbar for sunset walks. It takes longer to reach than islands near Malé, which keeps it quiet. Fulhadhoo is called the most romantic: tiny and undeveloped, with a long empty beach. Ukulhas is green, with a good lagoon and reef but no TMA (seaplane) platform. — [Beaches for Kings, Maldives for couples](https://beachesforkings.pages.dev/maldives/for-couples/); [Beaches for Kings, secluded](https://beachesforkings.pages.dev/maldives/secluded/); [Sygic Ukulhas](https://guides.travel.sygic.com/production/en/Ukulhas)
- Couples often spend most nights on a quiet local island and one night at a resort. — [Beaches for Kings, Maldives for couples](https://beachesforkings.pages.dev/maldives/for-couples/)

**Havelock**
- Radhanagar is about 2 km of soft white sand and the island's standout beach. Kalapathar is a quieter corner beach named for its black rocks. — [TravelTourister Havelock](https://www.traveltourister.com/?p=5782); [Trawell Havelock](https://trawell.in/andaman-nicobar/andaman-islands/havelock-island)

**Vietnam:** no quiet, warm beach area fits January on the Hanoi, Ha Long and Hoi An route (see section 1). For a slower pace, travel reporting points to Hoi An's old town and An Bang or Cua Dai as a place to visit rather than to swim. — [LocalVietnam Hoi An Jan](https://localvietnam.com/quang-nam/hoi-an/hoi-an-january-weather/)

### Inferences
- **Best quiet bases in Thailand:** Koh Yao Noi, then southern Koh Lanta, ahead of Railay. Railay was not directly sourced here; its day-tripper crowds are a gap. Kata Noi was not covered either.
- **Maldives for a couple:** Dhigurah (South Ari, with a sandbar and a bikini beach), plus optionally one resort night for alcohol and privacy. Fulhadhoo is the most secluded but the hardest to reach.
- **Sri Lanka:** base in Tangalle or Hiriketiya for quiet. Unawatuna is the better pick if calm, swimmable water matters more than quiet evenings.

### Gaps
- Kata Noi and Railay/Tonsai quietness were not covered by any source found.
- No source reported on Havelock's late-January crowding specifically.

## 4. Known downsides for honeymooners and transit time

### Takeaway
- **Thailand:** avoid Phi Phi, Maya Bay day-trip crowds and Patong.
- **Maldives local islands:** no alcohol, conservative dress outside bikini beaches, and long transfers to the quietest islands.
- **Andaman:** requires a Port Blair connection plus a 1.5-2.5 h ferry.
- **Sri Lanka:** combining the hill country with the beach means long drives.
- **Vietnam:** a multi-stop trip with a cold north.
- **Flights:** Delhi has a daily nonstop to Krabi (IndiGo), to Colombo (IndiGo, 6x weekly) and to Hanoi (Air India and IndiGo). No Delhi nonstop to Phuket or Malé was confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- **Maya Bay:** reopened 1 Oct 2026 and closes every year 1 Aug-30 Sep. Swimming is banned (wading to the knee only). Boats dock at Loh Samah and visitors walk in. The park fee is about 400 THB for foreign adults. — [Tisland Travel](https://www.tisland.travel/en/blog/maya-bay-otkryvaetsya-1-oktyabrya-2026); [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/maya-bay-thailand-sustainable-visitor-rules); [The Thaiger, swimmers fined](https://thethaiger.com/news/krabi/french-tourists-fined-maya-bay-swimming)
- **Maldives local islands:** no alcohol is sold on any of them. — [Beaches for Kings, Maldives for couples](https://beachesforkings.pages.dev/maldives/for-couples/)
- **Maldives transfers:** Dhigurah and Fulhadhoo take more travel to reach than islands near Malé. Ukulhas has no seaplane platform. — [Beaches for Kings, secluded](https://beachesforkings.pages.dev/maldives/secluded/); [Sygic Ukulhas](https://guides.travel.sygic.com/production/en/Ukulhas)
- **Andaman ferry:** Port Blair to Havelock takes about 1h30-2h35 (Makruzz about 90 min over ~57 km). Some sources list about 6 sailings a day, others name 06:20 and 14:00 departures. Fares are about ₹1,600-3,100. — [Ferryhopper](https://www.ferryhopper.com/en/ferry-routes/direct/port-blair-havelock); [Makruzz guide 2026](https://blog.makruzz.com/port-blair-to-havelock-ferry-guide-2026/); [FerryBooking.in](https://ferrybooking.in/port-blair-to-havelock)
- **Ha Long:** some budget junks have only reversible air-conditioning, which may be too weak for January cold. — [Bhaya Cruises](https://bhayacruises.com/blog/halong-bay-in-january/)
- **Delhi-Hanoi:** Air India nonstop since May 2026, 5x weekly, e.g. dep 01:20 arr 07:20. IndiGo has flown daily since 29 Mar 2026. — [VOV](https://english.vov.vn/en/travel/air-india-to-launch-direct-delhi-hanoi-flights-in-may-2026-post1274987.vov); [AeroRoutes IndiGo NS26](https://www.aeroroutes.com/eng/260310-6ens26int)
- **Delhi-Colombo:** IndiGo, 6x weekly from 29 Mar 2026. — [Travel Trends Today](https://www.traveltrendstoday.in/indigo-deploys-direct-flights-between-delhi-and-colombo); [AeroRoutes](https://www.aeroroutes.com/eng/260310-6ens26int)
- **Delhi-Krabi:** IndiGo increased from 4x to 7x weekly from Mar 2026. No Delhi-Phuket nonstop was found; the Air India booking widget showed none, and the Phuket nonstop found departs from Bengaluru. — [AeroRoutes](https://www.aeroroutes.com/eng/260310-6ens26int); [Travel Trade Journal, BLR-HKT](https://traveltradejournal.com/air-india-express-launches-direct-flights-between-bengaluru-and-phuket/)

### Inferences
- **Transit drag on a 9-day trip, lightest to heaviest:**
  - **Thailand via the Delhi-Krabi nonstop:** a short transfer to Lanta (road plus ferry) or to Koh Yao Noi (boat).
  - **Maldives:** Malé (likely via a connection, not confirmed nonstop from Delhi), then a speedboat to Maafushi or Gulhi, or a longer ferry or speedboat to Dhigurah, Ukulhas or Fulhadhoo.
  - **Andaman:** a flight to Port Blair (probably connecting) plus the ferry; the 06:20 or 14:00 sailing times can cost a day.
  - **Sri Lanka:** the Delhi-Colombo nonstop, then a south-coast drive. The Colombo-to-Tangalle drive time was not sourced, and adding Kandy or Ella adds several more transit days.
  - **Vietnam:** three regions (Hanoi, Ha Long, Hoi An) need an internal flight, a road transfer to Ha Long and an overnight cruise. This is the most fragmented option.
- **Maldives restrictions:** for a vegetarian, non-party couple, the no-alcohol rule is a minor or non-issue. The dress codes (cover up outside the bikini beach) matter more for the "romantic" feel.

### Gaps
- No sourced Delhi-Malé or Delhi-Port Blair nonstop schedule was found, nor a door-to-door transfer time for Malé to Dhigurah, Ukulhas or Fulhadhoo, or Colombo to Tangalle.
- No sourced Phi Phi nightlife or noise reporting was found (only the Maya Bay rules).
- Maldives local-island dress codes were not quoted from an official source.

## 5. Safety and ease of entry for Indian passport holders (as of Nov 2026)

### Takeaway
- **Thailand:** visa-free entry for 30 days since 15 Sep 2026.
- **Maldives:** free 30-day visa on arrival.
- **Sri Lanka:** a free 30-day ETA was reported from 25 May 2026, though some pages still list a $20 fee.
- **Vietnam:** requires an e-visa (blogs say USD 25 for 90 days; not confirmed officially).
- **Andaman:** domestic, so no visa.

### Cited Findings
- **Thailand:** Indian ordinary-passport tourists are visa-exempt for up to 30 days from 15 Sep 2026; longer stays need an e-visa via thaievisa.go.th. ## 6. Ranked verdict: romance and relaxation fit for 23-31 Jan 2027 (synthesis)

### Takeaway
1. **Maldives local island (Dhigurah, Ukulhas or Fulhadhoo, plus an optional resort night)**
2. **Thailand Andaman coast (Koh Yao Noi or Koh Lanta)**
3. **Andaman Islands (Havelock)**
4. **Sri Lanka south coast (Tangalle or Hiriketiya, without the hill country)**
5. **Vietnam (Hanoi, Ha Long, Hoi An)**

The top three are close on weather. The order turns on quiet and crowds, plus transit.

### Cited Findings
- (The ranking rests on the cited findings in sections 1-5; no new sources.)

### Inferences
1. **Maldives local island:**
   - **For:** peak dry season, calm seas, the year's best visibility, a ~28°C sea and 9-10 h of sun. Islands like Dhigurah are quiet and built around a sandbar and a bikini beach. Alcohol rules don't matter to a non-drinking couple.
   - **Against:** peak pricing, dress codes, and a longer transfer to the quietest islands. A Delhi nonstop was not confirmed.
2. **Thailand, Andaman coast:**
   - **For:** firmly dry, ~28°C sea, the easiest flight (the daily Delhi-Krabi nonstop), and genuinely quiet bases in Koh Yao Noi and Lanta.
   - **Against:** this is the No. 1 Indian Republic Day destination, falling exactly on 23-26 Jan, so hubs, Phi Phi and Maya Bay will be busy. Peak season everywhere. Stay away from Patong and Phi Phi.
3. **Andaman Islands (Havelock):**
   - **For:** an excellent January sea (dry, ~27°C, 15-30 m visibility), and Radhanagar is world-class. No visa.
   - **Against:** Andaman was a top domestic R-Day package in 2026, so a surge on 23-26 Jan is likely. There is a ferry dependency (1.5-2.5 h, fixed sailing times). The quiet resorts are fewer and pricier.
4. **Sri Lanka south coast:**
   - **For:** dry and sunny; Tangalle and Hiriketiya are quiet; a nonstop from Delhi.
   - **Against:** some open beaches have strong surf and currents, Unawatuna is noisy at sunset, and there is a long road transfer from Colombo. Adding Kandy or Ella brings misty, drizzly hill weather and long drives, which goes against "relaxed".
5. **Vietnam:**
   - **Against:** cold (10-19°C) and fog at Ha Long, a rough, cloudy sea at Hoi An, and three regions in nine days.
   - **For:** a romantic city-and-culture trip, but not a relaxed beach honeymoon on these dates.

- **Crowd tactic for every destination:** plan the most remote, quiet base for 23-26 Jan (the Republic Day surge) and keep any day-trips or sightseeing for 27-30 Jan.

### Gaps
- The ranking is qualitative. Prices, vegetarian food availability and resort-specific quiet were outside this note's scope and should be weighed using the parallel researchers' notes.

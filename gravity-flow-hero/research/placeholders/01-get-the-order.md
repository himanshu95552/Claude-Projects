## What the page says (what the image must be true to)
- Referral leakage is the order a center never receives, the first of three chances to lose an order. Three forms: the referral that goes elsewhere, the fax that never becomes an order, the referrer who quietly stops sending.
- Gravity closes it with easy ordering, live status and the report back the day it is signed. Three products: Gravity Doc (reads the fax, creates the order), Gravity Grow (referrer CRM, wins back who stopped), Gravity Referral (portal: upload once, see status, get report and images).
- Rules: no measured referral lift yet, so show how referrers are served, never a referral result. No numbers in art.
- Approved hook copy for the poster: "How many referrals did you lose last month? Most imaging centers can't say, because the answer is split across three systems."

## How I would explain it in one image
A line that should end in a node and does not. That is the brand device used as the idea itself. Then the variants differ in how much of the story they carry.

## Hero variants (all text-free except V4)
- **V1 The gap:** one line, one node, a visible break. Purest reading of the spec.
- **V2 Three ways to lose an order:** one line veers to another center, one stops at a fax that never becomes an order, one fades out as the referrer stops sending. Maps to the page's own three forms.
- **V3 The funnel:** eight referral lines, five arrive, three stop short; the ring round the order node is only partly lit. Shows a share lost without printing a number.
- **V4 Before and after:** the same line stopped, then carried through Gravity to a Lagoon order. Uses two micro labels (WITHOUT GRAVITY, WITH GRAVITY), so it is an exception to the no-text rule.

**Go to Krabi, Thailand. Spend four nights on Koh Yao Noi, two on car-free Railay and two in a pool-access room at Ao Nang, flying IndiGo's daily Delhi–Krabi nonstop both ways.** The plan costs about **₹1.92 lakh for two**. That total includes live flights of **₹69,192** (Google Flights, checked 9 Nov 2026) and estimated stays, boats, food and one island boat trip, and every one of the 8 paid nights is used. Thailand wins because it is the only option that does well on all four tests. Late January is the dry season with a ~28°C sea. There is a nonstop flight that lands at 12:20, so the first night counts. Ao Nang has a dense cluster of pure-veg Indian restaurants. And there are genuinely quiet islands you can hide on while the Republic Day rush (Sat 23 to Tue 26 Jan 2027) fills Phuket and Phi Phi. The **runner-up is the Andaman Islands (Havelock and Neil), at about ₹1.84 lakh**. It has the easiest vegetarian food of the five, the same calm, clear sea and no passport formalities. It loses on Republic Day crowds and on what a mid-range budget buys in Havelock hotels. The Maldives has the most beautiful sea, but its romantic quiet-island version comes in over budget, and its food is the hardest for vegetarians. Sri Lanka's classic hill-country train is still cut by cyclone damage. Vietnam is cold and grey in January. All hotel and food prices below are estimates, because no live late-January 2027 room rates could be read. Only the flight fares are live.

## Three of five destinations offer the right sea, and only one adds easy food and flights

On weather alone, the Maldives, Havelock and the Thai Andaman coast tie for late January. All three are in their dry season, with a sea of about 28°C. Phuket gets roughly 35–40 mm of rain on 4–7 days ([Weather2Travel](https://www.weather2travel.com/climate-guides/thailand/phuket.php); [Climate-Zone, 1991–2020 normals](https://www.climate-zone.com/climate/th/phuket)). The Maldives is at the core of its dry northeast monsoon, with about 9–10 hours of sun a day ([Holiday-Weather](https://www.holiday-weather.com/maldives/averages/january/)). Radhanagar beach on Havelock sees almost no rain in January ([Beach-Weather](https://beach-weather.com/Southern-Asia/India/Andaman-Islands/Havelock-Island/averages/Radhanagar-Beach/January/)). Sri Lanka's south coast is also dry, but some beaches there have strong surf and currents, and Tangalle is among them ([Beyond Wild Places](https://beyondwildplaces.com/best-things-to-do-in-tangalle/)). Vietnam is out of the running as a beach trip. **Ha Long sits at about 15–19°C with regular fog** ([Cruise in Halong Bay](https://cruiseinhalongbay.com/before-you-go/halong-bay-in-january)), and Hoi An's January sea is usually described as rough and cloudy ([LocalVietnam](https://localvietnam.com/quang-nam/hoi-an/hoi-an-january-weather/)).

The calendar matters more than the weather. **Republic Day 2027 falls on a Tuesday**, so many Indians will take a Saturday–Tuesday break exactly when this trip starts. In 2026, Thailand was the No. 1 international pick for Indians over that weekend ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/trends/story/republic-day-long-weekend-2026-from-goa-to-thailand-heres-where-indians-are-travelling-to-full-list-512647-2026-01-23)), and Andaman was among the top domestic packages ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/republic-day-long-weekend-triggers-robust-travel-activity-in-india-abroad-126012201176_1.html)). Thailand can absorb that surge and Andaman cannot. Thailand's crowds pile into Phuket, Phi Phi and Maya Bay, while Koh Yao Noi stays the quietest base on the coast, with nightlife that amounts to "just the sound of the waves" ([Visit Thailand Today](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/koh-yao-noi-guide)). Havelock is one small island, and every visitor there comes through the same Port Blair ferries.

Food decides the rest. The vegetarian notes score Andaman 4.5/5, Thailand 4/5 (Ao Nang 4, Koh Yao Noi only 2.5), Sri Lanka 4/5, Vietnam 4/5 and the Maldives local islands 3/5. On the Maldives islands, **Maldive fish (tuna) shows up in almost everything**, and paneer and dahi are scarce because everything is shipped in ([Maldives Traveler](https://www.maldivestraveler.org/maafushi-vs-dhigurah-vs-thoddoo-which-island-wins/)). Entry is easy almost everywhere. **Thailand has been visa-free for Indians for 30 days since 15 Sep 2026** ([Royal Thai Embassy New Delhi](https://newdelhi.thaiembassy.org/en/content/latest-update-on-revision-to-thailand-s-visa-exemp)), the Maldives gives a free visa on arrival, Sri Lanka uses an online ETA, and Andaman needs nothing. Vietnam alone needs an e-visa applied for in advance ([Happyfares](https://happyfares.in/blog/?p=3943)).

## Koh Yao Noi first, Railay second and Ao Nang last sends the crowds the other way

The route works because of when each place gets busy. The four Republic Day nights go to Koh Yao Noi, a quiet island in Phang Nga Bay. Two nights follow on Railay, a car-free peninsula you can reach only by boat, after the long weekend has emptied out. The last two nights are in Ao Nang, which has the most vegetarian restaurants and is 40 minutes from the airport, so the final morning is easy. There are only two mid-trip moves. Both are short boat or longtail hops timed between check-out and check-in, and both have published midday departures. The Koh Yao Noi to Railay speedboat (12:20 → 13:20) is the best-timed hop anywhere on the coast ([Visit Thailand Today, Krabi to Koh Yao Noi](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/krabi-to-koh-yao-noi)).

The route deliberately skips the earlier Phuket → Phi Phi → Krabi plan. That plan landed in Phuket at 10:40 PM, so the first night bought only a bed, and it put the couple on Phi Phi's busy Ton Sai strip during the busiest week of the season. The flights here are **IndiGo DEL 6:30 AM → Krabi 12:20 PM (₹18,318 per person)** and **IndiGo Krabi 1:20 PM → DEL 4:40 PM (₹16,278 per person)**, both live Google Flights fares for 23 and 31 January checked on 9 Nov 2026 ([Google Flights](https://www.google.com/travel/flights)). Delhi–Krabi became a daily IndiGo route in March 2026, so it is a running service, not a one-off ([AeroRoutes](https://www.aeroroutes.com/eng/260310-6ens26int)). The cost is the early start: leaving Delhi at 6:30 AM means leaving home around 4 AM. January morning fog at Delhi airport can also delay early departures. That risk was not researched here, but the plan carries 2.5 hours of slack before the onward boat.

## Pool-access rooms and early boats make the romance

The stays are chosen for privacy per rupee rather than for star rating. **Koyao Island Resort** on Koh Yao Noi has villas with their own yard and an infinity pool, and guests describe it as quiet and peaceful. It lists "from AU$116" (about ₹6,600) as an undated floor price ([TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com.au/Hotel_Review-g661285-d530441-Reviews-Koyao_Island_Resort-Ko_Yao_Noi_Phang_Nga_Province.html); [Trip.com](https://au.trip.com/hotels/ko-yao-noi-hotel-detail-1512853/koyao-island-resort/review.html)). Cape Kudu, "from $109", is the alternative on the same island ([Kayak](https://www.kayak.com/Ko-Yao-Noi-Hotels-Cape-Kudu-Hotel.880326.ksp)). On Railay, **Sand Sea Resort** sits right on Railay West beach. It is rated 9.2/10 and lists Indian cuisine and vegetarian options, though some reviews complain about breakfast ([Trip.com](https://us.trip.com/hotels/krabi-hotel-detail-1516706/sand-sea-resort/review.html); [Booking.com](https://www.booking.com/hotel/th/sand-sea-resort.html)). Railay Village Resort, with jacuzzi villas set among ponds, is the upgrade, but no usable price was found for it. For the finale, **Ao Nang Phu Pi Maan** has Premium Pool Access rooms on a quiet hillside, rated 9.1/10. Kayak's most expensive month there is February, at about $171 a night ([Expedia](https://www.expedia.com/Krabi-Hotels-Ao-Nang-Phu-Pi-Maan-Resort-And-Spa.h6598756.Hotel-Information); [Kayak](https://www.kayak.com/Ao-Nang-Hotels-Ao-Nang-Phu-Pi-Maan-Resort-and-Spa.623985.ksp)). None of these hotels publishes a honeymoon package. Put "honeymoon" on each booking and email ahead; documented perks such as Avani Ao Nang Cliff's bed decoration and cake come only through tour-operator packages ([Premier Holidays](https://www.premierholidays.co.uk/destinations/thailand-holidays/hotels/avani-ao-nang-cliff-krabi-resort)).

The experiences follow one rule: one early boat and one sunset per base, with the afternoons left free. Phra Nang fills with day-trip boats from mid-morning, so go before 10:00. The Hong Islands trip costs ฿1,200–1,400 per person shared, plus a ฿300 park fee paid in cash ([Visit Thailand Today, Things to do in Krabi](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/things-to-do-in-krabi)). A private sunrise longtail runs about US$231–274 on marketplaces, though it was not confirmed whether that is per boat or per person ([Expedia](https://www.expedia.com/things-to-do/from-ao-nang-private-luxury-longtail-boat.a47989678.activity-details)). It is the single most romantic upgrade, but it pushes the total past ₹2 lakh unless a cheaper Ao Nang room absorbs it. Phi Phi and Maya Bay are left out on purpose. Maya Bay allows wading only, with one-hour rounds, and day-trip speedboats arrive in force between 09:30 and 11:00 ([Visit Thailand Today, Phi Phi](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/guides/things-to-do-in-koh-phi-phi)).

## Vegetarian food is easy in Ao Nang and needs a plan on Koh Yao Noi

The weak point is Koh Yao Noi, which comes first. The island has no dedicated Indian restaurant. A few kitchens cook Indian food on request: Zazen is the most consistently reviewed, and Giant Summit has a few veg curries ([Bananarchy](https://bananarchy.in/guide/koh-yao-noi-indian-restaurants-where-to-find-indian-food-on-a-quiet-thai-island/)). Chaba Café and Garden Café are rated veg-friendly ([Restaurant Guru](https://restaurantguru.com/vegetarian-Ko-Yao-Noi-c93)). The fix is to email the resort before booking and get written confirmation of Indian vegetarian dinners. Avoid ordinary Thai curries, because **Thai red, green and massaman pastes almost always contain shrimp paste**, and "vegetarian" (mangsawirat) can still mean fish or oyster sauce. The words to use are "gin jay" (I eat jay, meaning vegan, no onion or garlic) and "mai sai nam pla, mai sai kapi" (no fish sauce, no shrimp paste). Look for the yellow jay flag outside restaurants ([Messy Vegan Cook](https://messyvegancook.com/traveling-vegan-in-thailand/amp/); [Yumsome](https://www.yumsome.com/mai-nam-pla/)). Jay food has no dairy, so paneer and dahi come from the Indian restaurants.

From Wednesday the food gets easy. Lonely Planet singles out the palak paneer at an Indian restaurant on Railay's Walking Street ([Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/thailand/andaman-coast/krabi-province/restaurants)). Ao Nang has **Govinda's (pure veg and vegan Indian)**, Hare Krishna Pure Vegetarian, Taj Palace (dosa and idli, with an extensive veg section), Ali Baba (veg thalis) and Delhi Haat, which is rated 4.7 from 358 Google reviews ([HappyCow](https://happycow.net/reviews/govindas-restaurant-krabi-79507?page=1); [HappyCow Hare Krishna](https://www.happycow.net/reviews/hare-krishna-pure-vegetarian-restaurant-ao-nang-463543); [Pickyourtrail](https://pickyourtrail.com/tourism/thailand/best-indian-restaurants-in-krabi); [VisitThailandToday](https://www.visitthailandtoday.com/restaurants/krabi/delhi-haat-multi-cuisine-fine-dine-indian-restaurant-and-bar-ao-nang-krabi)). Check each restaurant on Google Maps in January, because opening status changes.

## Andaman is the safe runner-up; the Maldives, Sri Lanka and Vietnam each fail one test

**Runner-up: Andaman, about ₹1.84 lakh.** Fly SpiceJet DEL 07:00 → Port Blair 12:30 via Kolkata (₹14,050), and come home on SpiceJet at 13:15 on the 31st (₹12,982), both live fares. On arrival day, take the **Makruzz 14:00 → Havelock 15:30**, the only realistic same-day sailing, and stay five nights. Move to Neil on Thursday 28 on the Makruzz 14:30 → 15:30, and to Port Blair on Saturday 30 on the Makruzz 12:15 → 13:15 ([Andaman World Travels](https://www.andamanworldtravels.com/port-blair-to-havelock-ferry); [Neil to Port Blair](https://www.andamanworldtravels.com/neil-island-to-port-blair-ferry)). That uses all 8 nights. Each ferry sector costs a couple about ₹4,000–5,500. Vegetarian food is effortless: Shakahaar Pure Veg on Havelock has about 600 Justdial ratings ([Justdial](https://www.justdial.com/Port-Blair/Shakahaar-Pure-Veg-Restaurant-Havelock/9999P3192-3192-190418213426-B9H9_BZDET)), and SeaShell is known for candlelight dinners ([Honeymoons.com](https://honeymoons.com/hotel/sea-shell-resort-spa-havelock)). Andaman loses to Thailand on three points. First, the Republic Day rush hits the same island and the same ferries. Second, romantic Havelock resorts cost more for less: Barefoot was ₹12,496 even for a June low-season night, before 18% GST ([Expedia.co.in](https://www.Expedia.co.in/Havelock-Island-Hotels-Barefoot-At-Havelock.h24226252.Hotel-Information)). Third, everything depends on fixed sailing times, and Radhanagar is open only from sunrise to sunset.

**Maldives: the dream that doesn't fit.** Live fares make it workable on paper: IndiGo's return via Bangalore and Kochi costs ₹35,573 per person, while Air India's nonstops cost ₹26,421 (DEL 8:00 → MLE 11:50) plus ₹28,129 (MLE 12:50 → DEL 17:30) ([Google Flights](https://www.google.com/travel/flights)). All three are live Google Flights fares for 23 and 31 January, checked on 9 Nov 2026; recheck them at booking. A quiet island like Ukulhas costs about ₹7,800–10,100 a night once 17% TGST and the green tax are added ([MIRA](https://www.mira.gov.mv/ContentItems/View/circular-issued-changes-to-tgst-rate)), plus about US$100 per person for the return speedboat. That puts the trip at **₹1.96–2.15 lakh on IndiGo, landing home at 12:50 AM, or ₹2.34 lakh and up on the nonstops**. The only version that fits comfortably is Maafushi, which sources call busy and "never secluded" ([Beaches for Kings](https://beachesforkings.pages.dev/maldives/for-couples/)). The rooms are guesthouse-level, and vegetarian meals depend on what each kitchen can manage.

**Sri Lanka** fails on "relaxed". **The Kandy–Ella line is still cut by Cyclone Ditwah**: trains run only from Ambewela or Nanu Oya to Ella, and the line is not expected back fully before early 2027 ([The Common Wanderer](https://thecommonwanderer.com/blog/kandy-to-ella-train-guide); [News 1st](https://newsfirst.lk/2026/09/06/train-services-between-peradeniya-and-colombo-fort-to-resume-next-month)). That breaks the earlier plan's train day and leaves 4–5 hour drives in its place. The quiet south coast has rough surf and no pure-veg restaurants found in Tangalle. **Vietnam** fails on weather: a foggy, 15–19°C Ha Long, a cloudy Hoi An sea and three regions to cover in nine days. It also needs an e-visa.

## Conclusion

What decides this trip is the calendar more than the destination. Seen that way, the Maldives' better sea counts for less than Thailand's ability to put a couple somewhere empty during the busiest Indian travel weekend of the winter. The same logic sets the order inside Thailand: the quietest place goes first, and the place with the best food and the shortest airport run goes last. The plan has one real risk, which is the hotel rates. The flights are live and the boats run on published timetables, but the roughly ₹69,000 of accommodation is estimated. The next step is to book the IndiGo fares now and get dated quotes from Koyao, Sand Sea and Phu Pi Maan for these exact nights, along with written confirmation of vegetarian meals from Koyao.

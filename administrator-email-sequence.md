# Administrator / practice or center manager: 4 variants with unique follow-ups and tracked journey link

Persona: COO / operations row of the Message House. Status: DRAFT. Lines are approved Message House wording arranged into new emails, so the marketing lead should sign off.

Each variant has its own angle and its own thread: Email 1 on Tuesday or Wednesday morning, the follow-up 3 to 4 business days later, the final follow-up 3 to 5 business days after that. Follow-ups reply in the same thread as their own Email 1.

Rules kept: "can", never "will". No RIS replacement claim. No ROI or net-benefit claim. No "cut staff". Staff are never the problem.

## How the link is used

**Format: a hyperlink on descriptive text, never a bare URL.** The brand guidelines specify plain-text-looking outbound email with one link, and a bare tracked URL with parameters looks like marketing and reads as a template. Each email has no more than one link.

**Placement:**

| Email | Link? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Email 1 (opener) | Yes | The buyer researches alone first (Message House §2.9), and the journey is the approved "demonstration" proof. A soft "see it" gives a reader who isn't ready to reply something to do. |
| Email 2 (follow-up) | **No** | It is a one-line question designed to get a reply. A link here competes with the question and lowers reply rate. |
| Email 3 (final) | Yes | It offers a no-meeting alternative: "if you'd rather look first". That makes a soft yes easy for someone who doesn't want a call. |

If your sending domain is new or still warming up, drop the link from Email 1 and move it to Email 2. Links in first cold emails are the most common cause of spam-folder placement.

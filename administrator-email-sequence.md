# Administrator / practice or center manager: 4-email cold sequence

Persona: COO / operations row of the Message House. Status: DRAFT. Lines are approved Message House wording arranged into new emails, so they need the marketing lead's sign-off.

Cadence: Email 1 on Tuesday or Wednesday morning. Emails 2 and 3 three to four business days apart, as replies in the same thread. Email 4 three to five business days after Email 3.

Rules kept: "can", never "will". No RIS replacement claim. No ROI or net-benefit claim. No "cut staff". Staff are never the problem.

# AN27 — Messaging Package

**v1.0 (final copy) · 23 September 2026 · Owner: Shamit Patel · Product: Gravity · Target: RSNA 2026**

> The ready-to-use message set for Gravity, built on the leakage argument. Every block traces to [[AN27 — Positioning and Messaging]] v1.7. The master message, promise line, descriptor and hero were approved on 23 September 2026 (positioning decision 18); the pillar 3 line, security wording, device wording and proof points were closed the same day (see [[AN27 — Open Items Register]]). v1.0 adds the referrer loop to pillar 1, the visit to pillar 2, charges from the report to pillar 3, and the search vocabulary per audience from [[AN27 — SEO and Keyword Map]].

## 1. The master message

> **Gravity is the Agentic Operations System (AOS) for your CRM, RIS and RCM. It gets the order, completes the exam and gets it paid, so no referral, no order and no revenue leaks away.** `LOCKED` 23 Sep 2026

Every other line in this package is a shorter or longer version of that sentence. One supporting line travels with it whenever the referring provider is in the room: **"Every referral captured. Every report returned."**

**How to read "no leaks."** It is the standard Gravity is built to, stated as a headline. In body copy, sales conversations and anything with a number, say what Gravity *stops* or *closes*, and how much. Never "guarantee," never "zero." Real operations have exceptions, and our own AOS page says the honest measure is a falling exception rate. A buyer who asks "zero, really?" should get: "Every leak is visible, and the routine ones close on their own. What's left goes to a person with everything they need to fix it."

**How to read "get the order."** The referring provider refers; what arrives is an order. "Referral" names the act, the relationship and the first leak. "Order" names the thing the center schedules, performs, delivers and bills. Never "get the referral" (positioning decision 25).

## 3. The three pillars

Each pillar is one leak and one verb. They are the same argument at three points in the journey.

### Pillar 1. No referral leaks: get the order

- **Claim:** Gravity gives your referring community better service than anyone else does, so the orders come to you. Every referral is captured, whether it arrives through the portal or by fax. The office's call is always answered. They see the order's progress in real time. The report and the images are back the moment the read is signed.
- **Support:** a referring physician portal with live order status, built-in fax, phone and text to referrer offices, report and image delivery to the referrer, and a CRM that shows who refers, who stopped, and why. Gravity's agents act as the center's liaison to every referring office, doing the clerical half of the physician liaison's job.
- **So what:** an open slot is sometimes a demand problem. Gravity fills the schedule from the top, not just the middle, and the referrer who gets the report back the same day sends the next order.
- **Lines:** "We don't just run your orders. We go get them." · "Every referral captured. Every report returned." · "We don't read the scan. We make sure it gets back."
- **Proof:** none measured for referral lift yet; modeled only. Never claim a customer result here.

### Pillar 2. No order leaks: complete the exam

- **Claim:** every order Gravity receives is digitized, scheduled and completed. Nothing sits in a fax tray or a work queue. The patient books by text or voice, checks in on an iPad without a clipboard, and the signed report lands in Gravity.
- **Support:** document and fax intake with automatic order creation, two-way patient text and the voice agent, scheduling and no-show refill, iPad kiosk check-in (identity, health history for the technologist, consents, payment), and readiness checks so the patient arrives cleared.
- **So what:** fuller schedules and shorter waits without adding scanners, rooms or schedulers.
- **Line:** "Your staff will never log into your RIS again."
- **Proof:** Tower Radiology, RSNA 2021: +7% exams a week in MRI, CT and mammography; 71% shorter wait for nudged patients; MRI slot utilization 65% to 70%. One person handles about 350 orders a day with Gravity.

### Pillar 3. No revenue leaks: get paid

- **Claim:** every completed exam gets paid, because coverage, authorization and estimates are settled before the exam, payment is collected at check-in, charges come straight off the signed report, and claims are worked to payment after it.
- **Support:** eligibility, benefits, estimates, prior authorization approved before arrival, payment at the kiosk, charges from the report, claim submission, remittance posting, patient balances; collections, refunds and statements in limited release.
- **So what:** fewer denials, faster cash, and problems fixed before they become write-offs.
- **Line:** "Every exam you complete, you get paid for." `LOCKED`
- **Proof:** none measured at a live customer yet for denials, A/R or collection rate. Say so if asked; the closer (slide 21) models it per exam.

### Why it works (support for all three pillars, never a fourth)

- **One system.** Phone, fax and text built in. Cloud, nothing to install. Runs in the browser on any computer; check-in runs on an iPad. Fewer vendors and interfaces to manage, and fewer to secure.
- **One set of data.** Three systems each see a third of the journey. Gravity sees all of it, referral to report to payment, which is what makes leakage visible: where orders stall, where capacity sits against unscheduled demand, denials by payer and exam.
- **Agents, not screens.** The work completes. People handle the exceptions.

## 7. Language rules for this package

- **Gravity is the subject.** "Gravity gets the order," not "our agent gets the order." When agents need naming, say "Gravity's agents."
- **Personas stay internal.** No Sasha, Sandra or Susan in any customer-facing asset, demo narration, booth, press or support macro. The voice agent's spoken name in the product is the one exception.
- **"Leaks" as a verb and "leakage" as a noun.** Always one of the three: referral, order, revenue. Orders leak; staff never do.
- **"Stops" and "closes" in body copy; "no leaks" in headlines.** Never "guarantee," never "zero."
- **"Agentic Operations System (AOS)"** spelled out on first use. Do not shorten to "agentic OS" in buyer copy.
- **"For your CRM, RIS and RCM"** is deliberately channel-safe. It reads as running across what they have, and the replacement claim lives in the on-ramp: "Until you're ready, it runs on top of them."
- **"Order," not "referral," as the object.** Get the order; every order can be lost three times; referral leakage; the referring community.
- **The report is delivered, never produced.** Whenever the loop is mentioned: "We don't read the scan. We make sure it gets back."
- **Search words in metadata, brand words in headlines.** Title tags, metas, one H2 and FAQs may say radiology, software, AI agents, analytics, referral management, patient scheduling, prior authorization automation. Headlines and body say imaging, system, agentic, insights, leaks. "Platform" and "AI-powered" appear nowhere.

## 5. Message by audience

The headline is the brand's. The subhead uses the words each audience searches and says (evidence in *AN27 — SEO and Keyword Map*).

### Who we are talking to

Each profile below is a short bio plus what that person cares about most. It is drawn from the table that follows, so the messaging and the bio stay in step.

| Audience | Short bio | What they like to focus on |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner / CEO** | Runs the imaging center and answers for its overall health: growth, margin and reputation with referrers. Sees the whole business, so is wary of vendor claims that don't hold at their size. | Capturing more referrals, filling more slots and collecting more of what they bill, without adding headcount. Wants proof the results are real at a center their size. |
| **CFO** | Owns the numbers: cash, collections, cost to collect and the business case for any new spend. Reads everything through ROI. | Revenue and the revenue cycle: fewer prior-authorization denials, faster A/R, lower cost to collect, and savings on staffing, software, outsourcing and IT. Wants one exam, itemized. |
| **COO / operations director** | Keeps the floor running day to day: phones, queues, scheduling, check-in and the team doing it. Protects staff workload and patient flow. | Order flow. Fewer no-shows, shorter phone queues, less manual RIS entry. Their first worry is what happens to their team. |
| **Marketing / outreach lead** | Builds and maintains the referral base, often through physician liaisons. Judged on referral volume and relationship health. | Referrals: knowing who refers, who stopped, and winning them back, with the report back to the office the day it's signed. Likely to say they already have a CRM. |
| **Revenue cycle lead** | Manages prior authorization, eligibility, claims and remittance posting. Lives in payer portals and denial queues. | Revenue, before the exam: authorization secured before arrival, denials fixed before they exist, and a team that works exceptions instead of portals. Wants to know it can answer the clinical questions. |
| **Scheduling lead** | Owns the booking process and the schedule: screening questions, reschedules, refills and call volume. | Order: patients booking by text or voice, no-shows refilled automatically, quiet phones, and never logging into the RIS again. Needs the screening questions to be right. |
| **IT director / security** | Responsible for the systems stack, integrations and protecting PHI. Cautious about anything touching the RIS, PACS or network. | The IT stack: one cloud system replacing phone, fax, text, interfaces and servers; HL7 and FHIR integration; AWS in US regions; a BAA with every customer; independent penetration testing. Asks whether cloud is secure enough for PHI. |
| **Channel partner (RIS / PACS)** | A RIS or PACS vendor whose customers we would serve. Cares about protecting their product and their account relationships. | Partnership, not replacement: their customers keep their system of record and Gravity writes to it. Lead with the partnership, never with "replace your RIS". |

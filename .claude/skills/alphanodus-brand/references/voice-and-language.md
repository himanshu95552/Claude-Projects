# Voice and language (AN27 Messaging Package v1.0)

Load before writing or editing any customer-facing words: headlines, body copy, emails, decks, microcopy, captions, alt text.

**Contents:** Voice · Master message · Three pillars · Lexicon · Copy blocks · Proof · Never say · Security wording

## Voice: analyst-adjacent operator

We speak as "we", operators who did the jobs before automating them, to "you, your team, your referring community". Plain, willing to concede, precise about claims, direct about consequences, structural before promotional. Explain the mechanism; competitors assert the outcome.

## Master message

Every other line is a shorter or longer version of this.

> Gravity is the Agentic Operations System (AOS) for your CRM, RIS and RCM. It gets the order, completes the exam and gets it paid, so no referral, no order and no revenue leaks away.

With the referrer in the room: "Every referral captured. Every report returned."

## Three pillars

One leak, one verb. The same argument three times, never three products, never colour-coded.

| 01 | 02 | 03 |
|---|---|---|
| No referral leaks | No order leaks | No revenue leaks |
| Get the order | Complete the exam | Get paid |

## Proof

- The one named outcome: Tower Radiology, RSNA 2021 Quality Improvement Award, presented by the customer's own physicians. +7% exams a week (MRI, CT, mammography) · 71% shorter wait for nudged patients · $2.3M added revenue · MRI slot utilization 65% → 70%. One person handles about 350 orders a day.
- Logos: current customers only. "Trusted by imaging groups across the US", no count.
- Testimonials credited by role and organisation type, never personal name.
- Referral lift and post-service revenue results are modeled, never claimed.
- "No leaks" is a headline standard, never a guarantee. Never "guarantee", never "zero".

## Never say

- "replaces your RIS" or "we will never replace the RIS" (channel-safe: runs on top of your CRM, RIS and RCM today; when you're ready, it can replace them)
- "cut staff", bots, robots, RPA
- "SOC 2 certified", "HIPAA certified", "more secure", ISO, HITRUST
- revolutionary, next-generation, cutting-edge, seamlessly leverage
- "sidekick" to scheduling audiences; "replaces your reps" to marketing leads; "drives efficiency" to CFOs
- Four Centers; "the front office of healthcare"
- any unsourced number
- emoji, exclamation marks, title-case headlines

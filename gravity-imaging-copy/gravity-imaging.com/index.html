What is Gravity?

Gravity is the Agentic Operations System (AOS) for your CRM, RIS, PACS and RCM. It gets the order, completes the exam and gets it paid, so no referral, no order and no revenue leaks away. Where there’s nothing to decide, Gravity just does it. Where there is, Gravity’s agents do the work and your people handle the exceptions.

What is an Agentic Operations System (AOS)?

Every order can be lost three times: before it arrives, before it’s scanned, and before it’s paid. Each of those leaks lives in a different system with a different team, so nobody sees the whole picture and staff patch the gaps by hand. Agents can now do that work. How does Gravity work with our RIS?

Gravity is the Agentic Operations System (AOS) for your CRM, RIS, PACS and RCM. It gets the order, completes the exam and gets it paid, so no referral, no order and no revenue leaks away. It works on top of the systems you already run.

Does Gravity read scans?

No. We don’t read the scan. We make sure it gets back. Radiologists read and sign the report; Gravity delivers the signed report and the images to the referring provider.

How many imaging centers use Gravity?

More than 460 imaging centers across the US run on Gravity, the Agentic Operations System (AOS) for imaging built by Alpha Nodus. Together they run more than 5.7 million imaging orders a year through one system, from the referral to the payment.

How is our data protected?

Hosted on AWS in US regions only. A HIPAA Business Associate Agreement with every customer. Independently penetration-tested every year.

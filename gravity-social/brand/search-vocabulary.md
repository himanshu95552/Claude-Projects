# Search vocabulary, hashtags and keywords

Source: AN27 SEO and Keyword Map v0.1 plus decision 27: **brand words in the headline and body, search words in metadata.** On social, "metadata" means hashtags, alt text, YouTube titles and tags, LinkedIn document titles, and at most one line of a long post.

"Platform" and "AI-powered" appear nowhere, not even in hashtags.

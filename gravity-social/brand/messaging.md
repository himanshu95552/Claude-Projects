# Gravity messaging for social

Condensed from AN27 Messaging Package v1.0 and Positioning and Messaging v1.7. Anything on social must trace back to a line here.

## The three-minute story (Winning Story v1.1 section 2, condensed)

An imaging center does three jobs: get the order, complete the exam, get paid. Every order can be lost three times: before it arrives, before it's scanned, after it's done. Each leak lives in a different system with a different team, so nobody sees one order from the referral to the payment and people carry it by hand. Airlines fill about 85% of seats; imaging centers about 65% of slots while patients wait weeks: not a capacity problem, a leakage problem. Agents can now do the work that needed judgment. The order is finished when the report and images are back with the referrer, which is how the next order arrives. Gravity is the Agentic Operations System (AOS) for your CRM, RIS and RCM. Your RIS records the work; Gravity does the work. Pick the leak that costs you most; we'll measure it with you, then close it.

Every post should be a slice of this story. Category details (the five tests, the comparison table, the maturity model, the nine questions) come from `brand/aos-definition.md`, verbatim.

## Master message

> **Gravity is the Agentic Operations System (AOS) for your CRM, RIS and RCM. It gets the order, completes the exam and gets it paid, so no referral, no order and no revenue leaks away.**

When the referring provider is part of the story, add: **"Every referral captured. Every report returned."**

## The argument in one breath

Every order can be lost three times: before it arrives (referral leakage), before it's scanned (order leakage), and before it's paid (revenue leakage). Each leak lives in a different system (CRM, RIS, RCM) with a different team, so nobody sees the whole order and people patch the gaps by hand. Agents can now do that work: hold the call, read the fax, work the portal. Gravity does it as one system, on one set of data.

An order is a loop, not a line: it is finished when the signed report and the images are back with the referring provider, because that is when the referrer decides where the next order goes.

## Three pillars

| Pillar | Leak | Verb | Replaces | Claim | Line |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Referral leakage | **Get the order** | CRM | Every referral captured, portal or fax. The office's call always answered. Live order status. Report and images back the moment the read is signed. You know who refers, who stopped, and why. | "We don't just run your orders. We go get them." |
| 2 | Order leakage | **Complete the exam** | RIS | Every order digitized, scheduled and completed. Patients book by text or voice, check in on an iPad, arrive cleared. | "Your staff will never log into your RIS again." |
| 3 | Revenue leakage | **Get paid** | RCM | Coverage, authorization and estimates settled before the exam; payment at check-in; charges straight off the signed report; claims worked to payment. | "Every exam you complete, you get paid for." |

**Why it works (support, never a fourth pillar):** One system (phone, fax and text built in; cloud; browser on any computer; iPad check-in; fewer vendors to manage and secure). One set of data (three systems each see a third of the journey; Gravity sees all of it). Agents, not screens (the work completes; people handle exceptions).

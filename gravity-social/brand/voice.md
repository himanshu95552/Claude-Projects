# Gravity brand voice for social

Source: AN27 Positioning and Messaging v1.7 §4 and §5, AN27 Messaging Package v1.0 §7, and the live-pitch lines carried in Deck Outline v2.1. If this file and those documents disagree, the documents win: fix this file.

## Who is speaking

Gravity, from Alpha Nodus. A team that did every job in an imaging center (front desk, scheduling, intake, authorizations) for a year before automating any of it. Engineers from companies that build large systems, working on the hard parts: handwritten faxes, real phone calls, payer portals with no interface.

The voice sounds like a founder who is angry about patients waiting six weeks for a scan and precise about why it happens. It is calm and specific, and it never hypes.

## Five traits

| Trait | Means | Sounds like | Never sounds like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Operator-literate** | We use the words people inside the center use: fax tray, work queue, payer portal, slot, backlog, auth, re-keying. | "A scheduler is typing an order from a fax into the RIS by hand." | "Streamline your workflows with cutting-edge solutions." |
| **Specific** | One exam, one slot, one number, one moment. | "A thirty-minute slot, on a specific machine, at 3:15 on a Tuesday." | "Unlock efficiency at scale." |
| **Calm confidence** | We state it and stop. No exclamation marks, no hype words. | "RIS records the work. AOS does the work." | "Revolutionary! Game-changing AI!" |
| **Honest about limits** | We say what we don't do and what isn't measured yet. | "We don't read the scan. We make sure it gets back." · "This part is in beta." | "Zero denials, guaranteed." |
| **On the staff's side** | Leakage is a condition of the systems, never the staff's fault. The staff hold the leaks shut today. | "Your people carry information between systems all day." | "Human error is costing you millions." |

## Sentence craft

- Short declaratives. One idea per sentence. Fragments are fine for rhythm: "Same scanners. Same referrers."
- Concrete nouns over abstractions: "the fax tray," not "inbound document workflows."
- Parallel triplets are the house rhythm: "Get the order. Complete the exam. Get paid." Use them, don't overuse them (one per post).
- Numbers as numerals in social copy ($39, 71%), except in spoken scripts, where they are written as they are said.
- American spelling. Oxford comma off in lists of three verbs, as in the approved lines.
- Questions the buyer can't answer are a strong opener: "How many referrals went elsewhere last month?"

## The two spoken images (the only metaphors we use)

1. **The empty seat at takeoff.** A slot is perishable inventory: if nobody is on the table at 3:15, that slot is gone, like an empty seat when the plane takes off. Airlines fill about 85% of seats; imaging centers fill about 65% of slots.
2. **The automation highway with exit ramps.** Routine work drives straight through. At an exit ramp a person steps in, with the whole file attached. "We don't throw work over the fence and say 'catch.'" "Your worklist becomes an exception list."

No other metaphors. Specifically no restaurant or fast-food analogy, no robots, no "digital workforce."

## Founder voice (Shamit Patel's personal LinkedIn)

The same rules, told in the first person, with more story:
- The six-week wait for his own scan, while centers had a third of their capacity unused. "That made me angry enough to leave my day job."
- The year volunteering in a center: "I hated most of it, and I understood all of it."
- Twenty years in wireless semiconductors, exits to Intel and Qualcomm: why hard engineering belongs in imaging operations.
- "Nobody starts a company because they're happy."
- "Just-in-time exists in every industry except healthcare."
- He names what isn't solved yet. That candor is the brand.
- He never names personas, never attacks competitors, and never states unverified numbers, even in a personal post.

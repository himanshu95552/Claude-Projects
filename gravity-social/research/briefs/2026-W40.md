# Research brief · 2026-W40 (28 Sep to 4 Oct) · phase: category seeding

**This week:** UnitedHealthcare's Surest plans start requiring prior authorization for outpatient CT, MRI/MRA, nuclear cardiology and PET on **1 October** ([UHC](https://www.uhcprovider.com/en/resource-library/news/2026/pa-surest-radiology-services.html)). It's a live example of the revenue leak that starts at scheduling, and it lands in our week. Meanwhile "agentic AI in radiology" is being defined almost entirely as image interpretation ([Radiology](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/10.1148/radiol.252730), [Radiology: AI](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/10.1148/ryai.250651), [Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/articles/10.3389/fmed.2026.1927284/full)), which leaves the operations side open for the AOS definition. On LinkedIn, imaging prior-auth posts from operators get single-digit engagement. The ones that did better were specific scenes and surprising "it was somewhere else" numbers, not vendor claims.

**Signal to ride:** the Surest prior-auth change on Oct 1 (revenue leak).

## What performed in our space (LinkedIn, past month, via Crustdata)

Sample: 8 prior-auth posts and 6 "imaging center" posts (the second search was noisy; 3 of 6 were off topic). Raw numbers only: we don't have these authors' medians yet.

- **Hiring posts** for prior-auth specialists drew the most engagement in the prior-auth sample (18 reactions, 6 comments). The workforce that does this work is on LinkedIn and engaged. It also confirms the AMA figure that 40% of practices employ staff who do nothing but prior auth (B05).
- **"For 1 in 5 women… we found qualifying mammogram evidence somewhere else"** (26 reactions, 3 shares): a surprising number, then "the evidence existed outside" the system that was looking. That's our argument exactly: each leak lives in a different system.
- **"The scan was ordered. The auth wasn't there."**: the best idea in the sample (in imaging a missing auth is a write-off, not a delay), undersold by a services pitch.
- **"Prior authorization is a workflow designed by people who will never have to run it."**: an operator's contrarian line, strong body, low reach.
- **"Not every finding deserves a second look. These do."**: a curiosity-gap list in seven words.

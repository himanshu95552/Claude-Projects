# Hook scoreboard

Kept by the report module's Hook Scorer (`gravity-report`), read by the research module every week. Covers the last 4 weeks of **published** posts, grouped by hook pattern, format, pillar, audience and platform. Figures come from Metricool analytics (`reports/metrics/`) and Calendly bookings tied to posts (`reports/outcomes.csv`); only aggregates go here, never names or company domains (this repo is public).

Hook patterns: Proof/result, Warning, Contrarian, Question, Number, Scene, Definition, Story, Comparison.

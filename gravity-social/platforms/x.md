## Rules

- At most 1 hashtag (2 during RSNA week). Hashtags go at the end or not at all.
- "Radiology" may appear in the body on X when speaking to the radiology community or trade press. Mark it in the draft's reviewer notes.
- "Agentic Operations System (AOS)" is still spelled out on first use in each post or thread. In a thread, part 1 is enough.
- A link in the post is fine on X (unlike LinkedIn), but keep it to the last part of a thread.
- No quote-dunking. No sarcasm about competitors, payers or staff.

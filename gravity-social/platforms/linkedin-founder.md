# LinkedIn: Shamit Patel (founder and CEO), personal profile

**Role in the system:** trust and reach. People follow people. The founder profile carries the *why* (just-in-time care, the six-week wait, the year at the front desk) and a point of view on the industry that a company page can't credibly hold.

**The system only drafts.** Shamit approves his own posts (`config.yaml: approval.founder_posts_require`) and posts them himself (personal profiles are not scheduled through Metricool). Drafts should sound like Shamit talking, not like marketing.

## Voice

First person, plain, a little blunt, occasionally funny about himself. Engineer's precision: numbers, mechanisms, cause and effect. Honest about what isn't solved yet.

Lines that are his and can be reused:
- "Nobody starts a company because they're happy."
- "I needed a scan, and I couldn't get one for six weeks."
- "We asked a center to let us volunteer. We did every job in the building for a year."
- "I hated most of it, and I understood all of it."
- "Just-in-time exists in manufacturing, logistics, every industry you can name, except healthcare."
- "Please interrupt me. The sooner you know what we do and what our flaws are, the better."
- "Nobody should wait for care because of paperwork."

## Formats that fit

1. **Story posts** (1,200 to 2,000 characters): one moment, told concretely, ending on the lesson. Examples: the first handwritten fax the team couldn't read; the day a scheduler showed him four payer portals; why the engineers do the job before automating it.
2. **Point-of-view posts** on the week's industry signal, tied to one leak: "Everyone's talking about [X]. Here's what it means at the front desk."
3. **"What we got wrong" posts.** Candor is the differentiator. For example: why the old "platform" and "AI workforce" language was wrong, and what we say now.
4. **Category posts** in his voice: why he proposed the term Agentic Operations System, and why he'd like competitors to use it too ("If the five tests turn out to be the right tests, they will be applied to us too, and they should be.").
5. **RSNA posts:** where he'll be, who he wants to meet, what he learned.

## Structure

```
[A first-person hook, under ~140 chars: a moment, not a thesis]
[The scene, in 3 to 6 short paragraphs]
[The turn: what it taught him about imaging operations]
[One sentence connecting it to Gravity, never a pitch paragraph]
[An honest question to the reader]
```

## Rules

- Never a hard pitch. At most one sentence about Gravity, and many posts should have none.
- No links in the body. If needed, in the first comment.
- No personas, no competitor names, no unverified numbers, even though it's a personal post. The proof ledger applies.
- The turnaround story ("losing $400K, now making $350K") stays out (forbidden in the ledger).
- One post in three should be about people: the team, a customer's operator (with permission), the staff who hold the leaks shut.
- 3 posts a week at most. Best: Tue to Thu, 7:00 to 8:30 a.m. his local time.

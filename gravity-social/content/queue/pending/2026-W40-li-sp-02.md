## Post

We named a new category this year, and I'd like our competitors to use the name too.

It's the Agentic Operations System (AOS). Here's why imaging needed a new word.

Every category of imaging software was built to store information so a person can act on it. The RIS holds the order. The RCM holds the claim. The work in between (the fax, the phone call, the payer portal) has always been done by people carrying information from one screen to the next.

That was the only answer, because the work needs judgment. Reading a handwritten order. Talking a patient through a reschedule. Working a portal that has no interface. Software couldn't do it. Agents now can.

When something new becomes possible, buyers need a way to tell the real thing from the relabeled thing. So we published five tests. My favorite is the third: what happens when the payer has no API? "We'd need them to build one" is a disqualifying answer.

We have an obvious commercial interest in this category, and I'd rather say that plainly. If the tests are right, they'll be applied to us too. They should be.

What test would you add?

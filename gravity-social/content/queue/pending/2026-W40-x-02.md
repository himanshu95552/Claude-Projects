## Thread

### 1
Everyone is selling AI to imaging centers. Here are five tests for whether a system does the operational work, or just helps your staff do it faster.

An Agentic Operations System (AOS) should pass all five. 1/7

### 2
Test 1: it performs the work, not the workflow.

Ask what share of prior authorizations complete with no human touch. An answer in "time saved" describes workflow software. 2/7

### 3
Test 2: it spans the whole order, from the referral to the payment.

A coverage problem found at scheduling gets fixed before the patient arrives, by the same system, without a person carrying it across. 3/7

### 4
Test 3: it works in systems it doesn't own. Payer portals, referring-office fax lines, a legacy RIS.

Ask what happens when the payer has no API. "We'd need them to build one" is a disqualifying answer. 4/7

### 5
Test 4: it scales with volume, not headcount.

Ask what happens to your operating cost if volume doubles next month. A seat-based license answers that badly. 5/7

### 6
Test 5: it escalates by exception, not by default.

Routine work completes on its own. Exceptions go to a person with the whole file attached. Ask for the exception rate, by workflow. 6/7

### 7
We proposed the term, so hold us to the same five tests.

RIS records the work. AOS does the work.

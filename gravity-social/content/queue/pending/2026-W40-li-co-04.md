## Post

Your referral data doesn't know about your denials. Your schedule doesn't know which referrer's orders keep getting rejected.

This year the journals started defining agentic AI in radiology. Read the reviews and nearly all of it is about the image: agents that chain the steps of reading and reporting a study.

That work matters. It's also half the building.

The other half is where the order lives. The fax that has to become an order. The patient who has to be called back. The payer portal with no interface. The report that has to get back to the referring office. That half loses orders every day, and nobody has defined what agents should do there.

So we wrote it down. An Agentic Operations System (AOS) is not diagnostic AI. It doesn't read the scan. It does the operational work around the scan, and it's judged on five tests: the work gets completed, across the whole order, in systems it doesn't own, scaling with volume rather than headcount, with people handling the exceptions.

The definition is in the first comment. Tell us what's missing.

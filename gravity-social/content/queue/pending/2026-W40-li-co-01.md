## Post

How many referrals went to another center last month?

Most imaging centers can't answer that. Not because anyone isn't paying attention. Because the answer is split across three systems that never talk to each other.

Every order can be lost three times:

→ Before it arrives. The referrer sends it elsewhere, or the fax is never worked.
→ Before it's scanned. It sits unscheduled, and the slot goes empty.
→ Before it's paid. A missing authorization, found as a denial weeks later.

The CRM sees referrals. The RIS sees schedules. The RCM sees claims. Nobody sees the whole order.

So here's an easier question. We'll share what you told us on Friday.

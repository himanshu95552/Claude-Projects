## Post

Your RIS records the work. An Agentic Operations System (AOS) does the work.

For thirty years, imaging software has been built to store information so a person can act on it. The RIS holds the order. The RCM holds the claim. Your team carries everything in between: the faxes, the phone calls, the payer portals.

An AOS is built to finish the action instead. Its agents hold the call, read the fax and work the portal. People handle the exceptions, with the whole file in front of them.

We proposed the term, so we published five tests for it. Hold every vendor to them, us included.

Swipe through the five. The full definition is in the first comment.

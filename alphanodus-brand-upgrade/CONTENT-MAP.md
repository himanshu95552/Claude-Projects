## 5. Message hierarchy (keep every page consistent)

1. **Headline claim:** No referral leaks. No order leaks. No revenue leaks.
2. **Category claim:** Gravity is the Agentic Operations System for imaging.
3. **Mechanism:** Agents do the work, in one system, on one set of data.
4. **Outcome:** Get the order. Complete the exam. Get paid.
5. **Proof:** 460+ centers, 5.7M orders a year, 50k+ exams a month.
6. **Belief:** Just-in-time care.

Voice cues from the site: short declarative lines, concrete numbers, "we don't read the scan, we make sure it gets back", staff doing exceptions only. Avoid the old-site language ("Sidekicks", "Human Intelligence Meets Advanced Automation", generic "unlock your full potential").

## The process (7 steps)

1. **Gate the list before writing anything.**
   - Send only Group A (Hunter verified). Hold Group B (12 catch-all, bounce risk) and Group C (2 rows pointing at the same mailbox for two different people).
   - Drop the two radnet.com contacts (enterprise, not the independent ICP).
   - Qualify the oddballs first: 14 Street Medical, ABQ Orthopedics, and the seven contacts on the currentclinic.com domain (one cardiology organization).
   - Cap at 2 contacts per organization, staggered 3 to 4 days apart, each with a different angle (611 MRI has six people on the sheet; emailing all six looks like a blast).
2. **Pick the seat, not just the person.** Priority is operators and finance (administrators and RCM directors reply 2 to 3 times more than physicians), then owners. Radiologists and medical directors are a later, different message. Marketers get no pitch.
3. **Research two facts per organization**: one about the center (sites, RIS, size, ownership) and one about the person's job. Two custom attributes lifted reply rate from 3.6% to 5.6% in the benchmark data. Anything not confirmed by a reliable page is flagged.
4. **Choose one formula per email** (see below), one idea, one proof point from an approved number or a public customer, and one soft interest CTA.
5. **Touch 1 rules (from the benchmarks):** 60 to 80 words, plain text, no link, no tracking pixel, no attachment, subject of 4 to 6 words, single interest CTA ("worth a look?"), reply-based opt-out and the postal address (CAN-SPAM).
6. **Sequence: 5 touches over about 40 days**, in the same thread where possible.
   - Day 1: observation, capability, interest CTA.
   - Day 4: a different angle, with peer proof.
   - Day 10: first link, the single stats page or /demo. **Check first**: the dossier says /demo asks for a real phone number and birthdate. Tell prospects to use a test order and their own number, never a real patient's details, until legal confirms.
   - Day 18: role-specific industry stat with its year label (for example AMA 2025, Experian 2025, CAQH 2024).
   - Day 28 to 40: short break-up note.
   - No cold SMS and no cold calls from the Sandra agent (TCPA; no B2B exemption).
7. **Measure and decide.** Track reply rate and bounce rate only (open rate is dead). With about 15 sends in wave 1 this is a learning round, not a statistical test: read every reply, adjust one variable at a time. Pause if hard bounces pass about 2% or any spam complaint appears. Healthcare pace: 10 to 12 sends per mailbox per day, warmed 30+ days.

## Shamit's sample: what to borrow and what to test

His sample (Doe Media, "this can't be a coincidence") works through a **name-on-an-object image** plus a playful interest CTA ("Odd or kinda genius?"). Three cautions from the research: images show no meaningful reply-rate effect in the 31M-email data, remote images are blocked by default in Outlook and Microsoft 365 (common at imaging centers), and the sample itself carries a tracking pixel, which cuts reply rate (4.4% vs 7.4% without).
- **Borrow:** short, specific, a light closing line, personal detail.
- **Test (separate small cell, touch 3 or later, not touch 1):** a self-hosted image of the prospect's center name on our "empty MRI slot" visual ("Tuesday 3:15, 611 MRI, open slot"), no tracking, no link. I can generate these per prospect.

## Deliverability findings from the .eml header

- DKIM signs with Google's default key (`alphanodus-com...gappssmtp.com`), not alphanodus.com, and DMARC is `p=NONE`. Set up custom DKIM and move toward `p=quarantine` before volume. Consider a separate sending domain so the primary domain stays clean.
- Shamit's signature uses a `bitli.pro` shortened tracking link. Remove it from cold sends; shared shorteners and rewritten links get quarantined by Microsoft Defender gateways.

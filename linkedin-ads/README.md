# LinkedIn and Google ads for Alpha Nodus (Gravity)

All creatives use the Alpha Nodus website brand (deep ink `#1B1430` with the site's violet glow, lavender buttons, Urbanist and Inter, the real logo) and real lit 3D illustrations. Tokens and logos are in `brand/`. Each ad comes in three formats: square layout A (headline first), square layout B in `variants-b/` (picture first), and landscape 1200 x 627 in `landscape/`. `creatives/compare/` shows six ads in all three. The 3D scenes (source and renderer) are in `scenes3d/`.

- `playbook.html`: the full plan (open in a browser). Also published as an artifact.
- `order-trace.html`: landing page for the free 30-minute Order Trace offer. Publish at alphanodus.com/order-trace and add the booking link.
- `creatives/simple/`: 16 simple question ads, 1080 x 1080 PNG, and `creatives/simple/landscape/` with the 1200 x 627 versions. `simple-ads-copy.csv` has the matching copy.
- `creatives/modules/`: 14 single-image ads, one per Gravity module (square, plus `landscape/` at 1200 x 627). `module-ads-copy.csv` has the matching copy.
- `creatives/documents/`: three 5-slide document ads (PNG slides and a PDF for LinkedIn Document ads).
- `creatives/video/`: two silent 18-second MP4s (1080 x 1080) and their frames.
- `google-search-ads.csv`: seven Google search ad groups (keywords, headlines of 30 characters or fewer, descriptions of 90 or fewer).
- `creatives/research-set/`: the pasted research built as written, with only untrue lines corrected: two single images, two 4-slide decks (PNG and PDF), a 24-second video (MP4 and frames), and `research-set-copy.md` with the posts, ad copy and video script. Button: Register. Link: alphanodus.com/events/rsna-2026 (RSNA 2026, Nov 29 to Dec 2, Chicago).
- `creatives/photo/`: 14 photo ads (1080 x 1080, plus `landscape/` at 1200 x 627) built from the supplied AI-generated photos in `photos/` (cropped JPEGs plus `originals/`). Same question, answer and role tag as the illustrated ads. More photos can be added to the playbook's Photo ads tab.
- `thought-leader-posts.md`: three posts, each with an optional customer-proof paragraph.

Rules for every file: customer proof (Iowa Radiology, Downtown Radiology) uses the figures published on alphanodus.com; a written yes from each customer is still the safer course. The "170% net income" and "$26 of every $100" figures on the site are modeled, so they are not used in any creative.

`creatives/scene/` holds the scene-style ads that need no photo. The scene ads that still had photo placeholders (founder post, video storyboard, checklist form, fax photo, product screen) are left out until real photos or screenshots exist.

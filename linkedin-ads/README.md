# LinkedIn and Google ads for Alpha Nodus (Gravity)

- `playbook.html`: the full plan (open in a browser). Also published as an artifact.
- `order-trace.html`: landing page for the free 30-minute Order Trace offer. Publish at alphanodus.com/order-trace and add the booking link.
- `creatives/simple/`: 16 simple question ads, 1080 x 1080 PNG. `simple-ads-copy.csv` has the matching copy.
- `creatives/modules/`: 14 single-image ads, one per Gravity module. `module-ads-copy.csv` has the matching copy.
- `creatives/documents/`: three 5-slide document ads (PNG slides and a PDF for LinkedIn Document ads).
- `creatives/video/`: two silent 18-second MP4s (1080 x 1080) and their frames.
- `google-search-ads.csv`: seven Google search ad groups (keywords, headlines of 30 characters or fewer, descriptions of 90 or fewer).
- `thought-leader-posts.md`: three posts, each with an optional customer-proof paragraph.

Rules for every file: customer proof (Iowa Radiology, Downtown Radiology) needs written approval first. The "170% net income" and "$26 of every $100" figures on the site are modeled, so they are not used in any creative.

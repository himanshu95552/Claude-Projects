Headline gradient: `linear-gradient(90deg, #C9B3FF, #67E3EE)`.
Hero background: deep ink with a violet radial glow at the top right and a faint white dot grid.

## Type
Headlines: Urbanist 600, letter-spacing -0.03em, line-height about 1.04.
Body: Inter 400 to 600. Labels and captions: JetBrains Mono, uppercase, wide tracking.

## Components
Primary button: radius 8px. On dark: lavender `#C9B3FF` with ink text. On light: violet `#6B3FE4` with white text.
Cards on dark: fill white at 5 to 6%, 1px border white at 14 to 16%.

## Gravity Grow post

Your physician liaisons do great work, but they can't be in every referring office at once.

When local orthopedic and specialty practices send out imaging orders, where do they go? If you can't see who stopped sending, that volume quietly leaks to competitors.

Gravity Grow keeps a record of every referring physician, shows who sends to you and who sends elsewhere, flags volume that is rising or falling, and runs fax and email outreach to the offices you want back. Gravity Referral gives those offices a portal with live order status.

Stop losing referrals to the clinic down the street.

## Gravity PriorAuth post

Do you get paid for every exam you perform? If your honest answer is "mostly", this is for you.

In a typical operation, revenue leaks out long before a claim is filed. If eligibility isn't locked down at booking, or a prior authorization is missing from the payer portal, you end up with denials and endless rework.

Gravity Estimate checks coverage and benefits, and Gravity PriorAuth handles authorizations, before the patient steps into the scanner. Where the work is routine, Gravity's agents read the faxes and work the payer portals. Your people review the work and handle the exceptions.

Book a working session.

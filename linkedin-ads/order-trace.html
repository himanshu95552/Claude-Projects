Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no cost and no obligation.

Do I have to share patient information?

No. Describe the order in general terms, such as "an MRI referred by fax that needed a prior authorization." We do not need names or dates of birth.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It is a conversation, on video or by phone.

Is this a sales pitch?

No. At the end we ask whether you would like to see how Gravity handles the steps where your order stalled. If you say no, we stop.

Who runs it?

A member of the Alpha Nodus team who has worked with imaging-center operations.

Can I bring my team?

Yes. Many centers bring the front desk lead or the billing lead.

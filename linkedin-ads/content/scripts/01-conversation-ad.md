# Conversation ad script (LinkedIn message ad)

Sender: a named person at Alpha Nodus (photo + name), not "Alpha Nodus Team".
Audience: owners, administrators, practice managers, billing leads at US imaging centers.
Button text must come from LinkedIn's list. Confirm character limits in Campaign Manager.


---

## Opening message

**Subject:** One question about your imaging center

Hi {first_name},

Quick question, no pitch. Where does the most work get lost at {company}?

Pick whichever is closest.

**Buttons:**
1. Orders don't make it in
2. Scheduling falls apart
3. Insurance says no
4. All of the above

---

## Branch 1. "Orders don't make it in"

Thanks. That's usually one of two things: faxes sitting unread, or referring offices quietly sending patients somewhere else. Often both, and neither shows up in a report.

The simplest way to see it is to follow one order from the fax to the payment. We run a free 30-minute order trace where we do that together. Describe one order in general terms (no patient names), and you keep the notes.

**Buttons:**
- Book an order trace → landing page (order-trace)
- Read: where an order gets lost → article 01
- Not now

## Branch 2. "Scheduling falls apart"

Thanks. Usually it's gaps in the schedule that nobody has time to fill, or patients who need three calls before they book.

One center we work with, Downtown Radiology, saw self-booking go from 19.7% to 43.8% over a year. More than half of patients who got a text booked themselves. Here's the full story.

**Buttons:**
- Read the Downtown Radiology story → article 07
- Book an order trace
- Not now


## Branch 3. "Insurance says no"

Thanks. Denials land weeks after the visit, when nobody remembers the details. They usually trace back to something that was knowable at booking: wrong payer, missing authorization, no estimate.

Here are five checks to make at the moment a patient books.

**Buttons:**
- Read the five checks → article 02
- Book an order trace
- Not now

## Branch 4. "All of the above"

That's more common than you'd think. Each leak lives in a different system, so each team can say it's doing fine.

The order trace follows one order through all of them. Thirty minutes, free, notes are yours.

**Buttons:**
- Book an order trace
- Download the checklist → checklist landing page
- Not now

## Compliance notes

- Do not mention patient data or specific payers.
- Do not promise results. Use only customer outcomes as stated on the customer pages.
- Frequency: one conversation ad per member per 30 days (LinkedIn default); do not stack with other message ads.

# Product demo video script: "One order, start to payment"

Length: 90 seconds, with 30-second and 15-second cutdowns.
Format: screen recording of the Gravity demo environment, with simple captions. No stock footage, no voice-over music bed that competes with speech. Square 1:1 and 16:9 versions.
Data: demo data only. No real patient information appears on screen, ever.

Before shooting: confirm with product which screens are current and that the demo data is synthetic.

---

## 90-second cut

| Time | On screen | Caption / voice |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | A faxed order, slightly crooked, handwritten note in the margin | "This is how most orders arrive." |
| 0:05–0:15 | The fax is read into a draft order: patient, exam, provider fields filled; one field flagged for a person | "Gravity reads it into an order. Anything unclear goes to a person." |
| 0:15–0:25 | Staff member confirms the flagged field; order moves to ready | "A person checks the exception, not the whole fax." |
| 0:25–0:38 | Patient's phone: text with open slots; patient taps one | "The patient gets a text with open times and books from their phone." |
| 0:38–0:50 | Coverage check and authorization status shown on the order; status moves to approved | "Coverage and authorization are handled before they arrive." |
| 0:50–1:00 | Patient pays their share on the phone | "They see what they owe and pay it ahead of the visit." |
| 1:00–1:10 | Signed report returns to the referring office, with a timeline of every step on the order | "The signed report goes back to the person who ordered it. Every step is on the timeline." |
| 1:10–1:20 | Claim paid; order marked complete | "The claim is worked until it's paid." |
| 1:20–1:30 | End card: logo, "See your own order traced. Free, 30 minutes." and URL | CTA |

## 30-second cut

0:00 fax → 0:06 draft order with one flagged field → 0:12 patient books by text → 0:19 coverage and authorization approved → 0:25 timeline and paid → end card.

## 15-second cut

0:00 fax → 0:04 draft order → 0:08 patient books by text → 0:12 end card: "One order, start to payment."

## Rules

- Captions burned in (most viewers watch muted). Large type, brand font, no more than 8 words per card.
- Show the exception being handled by a person at least once. It is the credibility moment.
- No claims about speed or savings in the video. Customer numbers belong in static ads with sources.
- End card button on LinkedIn: Learn more, or Request demo.
- Alternate end card for retargeting: "Book your demo" with the 30-minute agenda.

# Press and credibility posts (confirm wording before use)

Only use if Alpha Nodus confirms each item. Flagged items are not in the site facts I fetched.

1. **RSNA report or journal item.** The site references an RSNA 2021 award in its history. Do not present it as Alpha Nodus's own unless confirmed. Ask: who won, for what, and may we say so?
2. **Journal article.** If a published article exists, quote its title and journal exactly, and link it. Ask for the citation.
3. **KLAS or analyst mention.** Only post after receiving the report's wording and permission to quote it. Ask: is there a KLAS or similar report, and what may we quote?
4. **Security and compliance.** Post only what the Trust Center states. Do not mention SOC 2 unless Alpha Nodus confirms it.

Template for a confirmed item:
"[Short factual statement, exact wording from the source]. [Link]. [One line on why it matters to an imaging center.]"

# Employee advocacy kit

Purpose: give team members posts they can edit and publish in their own voice. Keep posts short, personal and specific. Do not copy these word for word; change at least the first line.

Rules for the team:
- Never name a patient, a payer contract or a customer who hasn't approved it.
- Only repeat customer numbers that appear in the published customer stories.
- No claims about savings that aren't from a customer story.
- Link in the first comment, not the post.

---

## Six starter posts

1. **From a team member who works with customers:** "The most common thing I hear on a first call: 'Our faxes sit.' It's not a complaint, just how work moves. We built a free 30-minute order trace to look at exactly that. Link in the comments."
2. **From product:** "We don't read the scan. We make sure it gets back. That's the line Gravity doesn't cross, and why radiologist-owners keep their hands on the read."
3. **From someone in implementation:** "Day one at Iowa Radiology: about 430 orders waiting. Day three or four: gone. I'm still thinking about the specialists who'd been carrying that for months."
4. **From sales:** "Before you hire another scheduler, write down where your current scheduler's day goes. We turned that into fifteen questions. Link in comments."
5. **From a founder:** "Why we built Gravity as one system instead of one more tool: every leak lives in a different system, so every team can say it's doing fine."
6. **From anyone going to RSNA:** "I'll be at booth 6537 in the North Hall. If you're an imaging center owner going, bring one order and a question."

## Comment replies (to use under your own posts)

- "Do you integrate with our RIS?" → "Gravity runs alongside the systems you already use. Which one are you on? I can check specifics."
- "Is this AI that reads scans?" → "No. It doesn't touch the read. It handles the work around it: orders, booking, coverage, payment, and sending the signed report back."
- "What does it cost?" → "Starts at $1,000 a month, depending on plan and exam volume."
- "How long to go live?" → "It depends on your setup. I'd rather not guess in a comment, but happy to walk through it."
- "Can we try it?" → "The easiest first step is a free 30-minute order trace. We trace one of your orders together. Link below."
- "We already use [vendor]." → "Understood. Many centers do. The trace shows which steps still wait on a person, whichever system you're on."

## Cadence

Two posts per person per week. Reactions and short comments on the company page count. Do not require people to post.

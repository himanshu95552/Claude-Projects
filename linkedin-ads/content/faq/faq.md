# Frequently asked questions

Answers use only what the Alpha Nodus website states. Items marked "confirm" need a check with the team.

**Does Gravity read scans or write reports?**
No. It doesn't touch the clinical read. It handles the work around it: orders, booking, coverage, authorization, payment, and returning the signed report.

**What does it do by itself, and what goes to a person?**
Routine work runs on its own (reading a fax into an order, checking benefits, texting open slots, submitting authorizations, sending reports). Anything it can't resolve goes to a person in a shared list, assigned by capacity and skill.

**Can we control how much a person reviews?**
Yes. Each center chooses. For order intake, review every order or only the unusual ones. For authorizations, a record can be set to review every run, finish on its own, or be turned off.

**Can I see what it did on an order?**
Yes. Changes, communications and automated runs are logged, and each order has a timeline.

**What systems does it work with?**
It runs alongside the systems you already use. See alphanodus.com/solutions/ris-ehr-integration.

**How does a demo work?**
Thirty minutes on Google Meet. First 5 minutes: you describe where work slips. Then a fax read into an order, a patient booking from a text, coverage and payment, and the claim worked to paid. Last 5 minutes: your questions.

**What is the order trace?**
A free 30-minute session where we walk one of your orders from referral to payment and mark every stall. You describe the order in general terms; no patient names. You keep the notes. 

**What does it cost?**
Pricing starts from $1,000 a month, based on plan and monthly exam volume.

**Do you need to add staff?**
Iowa Radiology reports they did not add staff after Gravity went live.

**How do patients book?**
They get a text with open times and book from their phone. At Downtown Radiology, 56.5% of texted patients booked themselves.

**Is patient data secure?**
Gravity Trust covers single sign-on, user permissions and an audit trail. See alphanodus.com/products/gravity-trust.

**Who is it for?**
Independent imaging centers in the United States: owners, administrators, practice and operations managers, billing and revenue cycle leads, and radiologist-owners.

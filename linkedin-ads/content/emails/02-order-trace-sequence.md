# Email sequence 2: around the free 30-minute order trace

Trigger: someone books an order trace. Four messages. Merge fields: {first_name}, {company}, {call_time}, {sender_name}.


---

## Email 1. Booking confirmation (immediately)

**Subject:** Your order trace on {call_time}
**Preview:** What to bring, and what to expect.

Hi {first_name},

You're booked for {call_time}. Here's how it works.

**What to bring:** one order from the past week or two that took longer than it should have. Pick whichever one you'd rather not have handled the way it was. You don't need to prepare anything else.

**Keep it general.** Describe the order the way you'd describe it to a colleague: "an MRI, referred by fax, needed a prior authorization, patient was rescheduled once." No names or dates of birth.

**Who should join:** you, plus whoever knows the steps best, such as your front desk lead or your billing lead. One person is enough.

**What we'll do:** walk the order from the referral to the payment and mark every place it waited on a person, a fax or a phone call. You'll leave with a written list of where it stalled.

If you need to move the time, use the link in your calendar invite.

{sender_name}
Alpha Nodus

---

## Email 2. Day before

**Subject:** Tomorrow: your order trace
**Preview:** One order is all you need.

Hi {first_name},

A reminder that we're talking tomorrow at {call_time}.

If you haven't chosen an order yet, a good one is any where a patient waited, a claim came back, or someone said "why did that take so long?" The messier, the better.

See you then.

{sender_name}

---

## Email 3. If they miss it (an hour after)

**Subject:** Sorry we missed each other
**Preview:** Want to pick another time?

Hi {first_name},

Looks like today didn't work out, and that's fine. Things happen at imaging centers.

Here's the link if you'd like to pick another time: alphanodus.com/order-trace. If it's easier to tell me what's going on first, just reply.

{sender_name}

---

## Email 4. After the call (same day)

**Subject:** Your order trace notes
**Preview:** Where the order stalled.

Hi {first_name},

Thanks for your time today. Here are the notes from your order, as promised.

**The order we traced:** [one-line description, no patient details]

**Where it stalled:**
1. [Step, who touched it, how long it waited]
2. [Step, who touched it, how long it waited]
3. [Step, who touched it, how long it waited]

**What would have changed it:** [one sentence per stall]

These are yours to use whether or not we ever work together.

If you'd like to see how Gravity would handle those steps, I can show you on a 30-minute demo, using the same order. If not, no worries at all.

{sender_name}

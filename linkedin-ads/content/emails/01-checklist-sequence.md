# Email sequence 1: after someone downloads the checklist

Trigger: LinkedIn lead form "Score your center before the next hire". Five emails over about three weeks. Send from a named person, not a no-reply address. Keep every email short. Merge fields: {first_name}, {company}, {monthly_exams} (from the form).

Before sending: confirm the free order trace terms and booking link (alphanodus.cal.com or your landing page).

---

## Email 1. Immediately

**Subject:** Your checklist
**Preview:** Fifteen questions, about ten minutes.

Hi {first_name},

Here's the checklist you asked for: [link to PDF].

It's fifteen yes-or-no questions in three groups: where orders disappear, where visits stall, and where money slips. Answer them with whoever runs your front desk and billing if you can. It goes faster with two people.

You don't need to score it. Just circle every "no" or "not sure". Those are the places worth looking at.

If you'd like a second pair of eyes after you've done it, reply to this email and tell me which question surprised you most.

{sender_name}
Alpha Nodus

## Email 2. Two days later

**Subject:** How to read your answers
**Preview:** The "no" answers tell you where to look.

Hi {first_name},

A quick note on the checklist.

Don't add up a score. Look at which group your "no" answers cluster in.

- Mostly in the first group (orders disappear): your problem is probably intake and referrals.
- Mostly in the second (visits stall): booking, authorizations and no-shows.
- Mostly in the third (money slips): coverage, estimates and denials.

Then pick one order from last week that touched those steps and follow it from the referral to the payment. Write down every place it waited on a person.

That one order will tell you more than the checklist did.

{sender_name}

---

## Email 3. Five days later

**Subject:** The leak most centers look at last
**Preview:** It's the one that doesn't show up in any report.

Hi {first_name},

Most teams start with the leak they can see: denials, because they land in a queue.

The one that's harder to see is upstream. Orders that never arrive because a referring office quietly sent them elsewhere. Orders that arrive and sit for days because the fax hasn't been read yet. Nothing gets recorded when an order doesn't come in, so nothing shows up in a report.

If you only fix one thing this month, I'd look at how long your oldest unentered order has been waiting. It takes five minutes to find out, and it usually tells you something.

{sender_name}

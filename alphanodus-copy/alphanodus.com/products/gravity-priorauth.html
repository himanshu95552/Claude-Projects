Questions

Questions about prior authorization software

What is Gravity PriorAuth?

Gravity PriorAuth makes sure every exam that needs a prior authorization has one. Rules say which exams need one for each payer and code, requests are submitted and their status checked on the payer’s portal without retyping the order, and every authorization sits on the order with its status, number and dates.

What happens to my team?

Gravity takes over the tasks, not the people. The routine work completes on its own, so your team can give patients the attention the paperwork used to take.

How does Gravity know which exams need a prior authorization?

From auth rules for each payer and code, global and local, kept with admin approval, verification and renewal, so staff know right away which exams need an authorization.

Can we choose which authorizations run without us?

Yes. Every agentic flow has a review setting. You choose it. Each record is set to review every run, let it finish on its own, or off.

Is the referring office told what an order is missing?

Yes. Referring coordinators are told what the order still needs, such as clinical notes or an authorization, and what is delaying care, and the center’s marketers are told when a referring office owes them. Staff can also ask the office for the progress note or the prior auth from the order.

How is our data protected?

Hosted on AWS in US regions only. A HIPAA Business Associate Agreement with every customer. Every sub-processor used in Gravity has a BAA with Alpha Nodus. Independently penetration-tested every year. Data encrypted at rest with per-tenant keys. MFA on every production login, SSO on Enterprise. Controls monitored continuously, with a public Trust Center.
